**Goal:** By the end of this short session you will have a tidy Instagram and Facebook (or TikTok) business profile, a free Canva account, Meta Business Suite connected, and a one-page brand sheet you will reuse all course. Almost no reading, you set each thing up as you go.

## What you'll be able to do

- Set up or clean up a business profile on Instagram and on Facebook or TikTok
- Create a free Canva account and find your way around it
- Connect your accounts in Meta Business Suite so you can post and schedule from one place
- Write a one-page brand sheet that keeps every future post consistent

## Before you start (about 10 minutes)

- Have your phone and a laptop ready, plus the email you want to use for business.
- Warm-up: on your phone, open the app store and make sure Instagram, Facebook and Canva are installed and updated.

## Try it 1 - Set up or tidy one Instagram business profile

**Why it matters:** a clear profile is the first thing a new follower judges. It is a free win before you post anything.

**Do this now:**
1. Open Instagram, go to your profile, tap the menu, then Settings and privacy, then Account type and tools, and switch to a Professional (Business) account.
2. Set a clean profile photo (a logo or a clear headshot) and a username that is easy to spell.
3. Write a bio in three short lines: who you help, what you offer, and one call to action (for example "DM 'PRICE' to order").

**You've got it when:** your Instagram shows a business profile with a clear photo, readable username, and a three-line bio.

## Try it 2 - Set up or tidy one Facebook Page (or a TikTok profile)

**Why it matters:** having a second platform doubles your reach and lets you test where your audience responds best.

**Do this now:**
1. On Facebook, tap the menu, then Pages, then Create, and make a Page with your business name and category.
2. Add the same profile photo you used on Instagram, plus a cover image, so the two match.
3. Fill in the About section: a one-line description, your location (for example Yenagoa or Lagos), and your contact.

**You've got it when:** you have a Facebook Page (or a TikTok profile) that visually matches your Instagram.

## Try it 3 - Create your free Canva account

**Why it matters:** Canva is where you will design every post. Getting comfortable now saves hours later.

**Do this now:**
1. Go to https://www.canva.com and sign up free with your business email.
2. In the search box, type "Instagram post" and open any template to see how editing works.
3. Change one text box and one colour, then close it without saving. You are just exploring the buttons.

**You've got it when:** you can open a template, edit text, and change a colour without feeling lost.

## Try it 4 - Connect Meta Business Suite

**Why it matters:** Meta Business Suite lets you post and schedule to Instagram and Facebook from one screen, for free.

**Do this now:**
1. On a laptop, go to https://business.facebook.com and log in with the account that owns your Page.
2. Follow the prompts to connect your Facebook Page and your Instagram business account.
3. Open the Planner (calendar) tab once so you know where scheduling lives. You will use it in Week 3.

**You've got it when:** Meta Business Suite shows both your Facebook Page and Instagram connected.

## Try it 5 - Build your one-page brand sheet

**Why it matters:** a brand sheet keeps every post looking like it came from the same place. Consistency is what makes a small account look professional.

**Do this now, write one line each in a doc or Canva:**
1. **Name:** your business or personal brand name.
2. **Colours:** pick 2 to 3 colours (use https://www.canva.com colour tools if unsure) and write their codes.
3. **Fonts:** choose a font pairing, one bold font for headings and one simple font for body text.
4. **Who you serve:** one sentence naming the exact person you help.
5. **Voice in 3 words:** for example "friendly, clear, confident".

**You've got it when:** you have one page listing name, colours, fonts, audience, and three voice words.

### Watch out for

- A personal profile left as-is instead of switching to a business account (you lose free insights).
- Mismatched photos across platforms, which makes you harder to recognise.
- Picking five colours instead of three. Fewer choices look cleaner and are easier to stay consistent with.

## You are ready when

- [ ] Instagram is a business profile with photo, username and three-line bio
- [ ] A Facebook Page (or TikTok profile) is set up and matches your Instagram
- [ ] A free Canva account is created and you can edit a template
- [ ] Meta Business Suite shows Instagram and Facebook connected
- [ ] A one-page brand sheet exists: name, colours, fonts, audience, three voice words
