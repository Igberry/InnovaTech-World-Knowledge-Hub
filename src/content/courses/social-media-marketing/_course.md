**Turn posting into a plan.** Social Media Marketing is a hands-on, 6-week course where you build real content,
grow a real audience, and run a real campaign, using only free tools. There is very little reading: every week
you make something and get feedback.

## Who this is for

Anyone who wants to market a business, a brand, or themselves on social media: small business owners,
freelancers, aspiring social media managers, and creators. You need basic phone and internet skills and a
willingness to post and learn. No marketing background needed.

## What you'll be able to do

- Pick the platforms where your audience actually is, and skip the rest
- Write hooks and captions that stop the scroll and invite action
- Design on-brand posts, carousels, and stories in Canva
- Plan, batch, and schedule content so you stay consistent
- Grow followers through genuine engagement, not tricks
- Boost a post safely with a small budget and understand the numbers
- Read your insights and run a measurable one-week campaign

## The 6-week plan

| Week | Topic | Main deliverable |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Setup: profiles, Canva, Meta Business Suite, brand sheet | Accounts ready and a one-page brand sheet |
| 1 | Platforms and your audience | A written audience persona and 2 chosen platforms |
| 2 | Content that stops the scroll | Hooks, one idea in 3 formats, strong captions |
| 3 | Content calendar and scheduling | A 2-week calendar with 6 posts scheduled |
| 4 | Growing and engaging an audience | A hashtag set and a daily engagement routine |
| 5 | Boosting and intro to ads | One boosted post with a budget and objective |
| 6 | Analytics and capstone campaign | A measured one-week campaign, presented |

## Weekly time (about 6-8 hours)

| Activity | Hours |
|---|---|
| Follow the lesson and do the Try-it tasks | 3 |
| Make content / practice | 2 |
| Weekly assignment | 1.5 |
| In-person mentoring | 1.5 |
| **Total** | **~8** |

## How mentoring works

Each week has a 90-minute live group mentoring session: review your posts, plan content together, and get the
brief for the next assignment. You do most of the making on your own between sessions.

## Assessment and certification

| Component | Weight |
|---|---|
| Weekly assignments (Weeks 1-5) | 45% |
| Capstone campaign and presentation (Week 6) | 35% |
| Attendance and consistency | 20% |

A completion certificate is awarded at 70% or above overall.

## Honest outcomes

This course gives you real, practical social media marketing skills and a portfolio of work you can show. It
does **not** guarantee a job, clients, or income; that depends on your skills, effort, and the market. Platforms
and their free features change often, so the course teaches durable thinking (audience, content, consistency,
measurement) over any one app's buttons.

## Facilitator Notes

The two things that make or break results are consistency (Week 3) and genuine engagement (Week 4); spend extra
time there. Have learners post real content in sessions and critique it together. Keep reinforcing platform rules
(no buying followers, no spam) and privacy. Because features change, teach the thinking, not the menus, and have
learners confirm current options themselves.
