**Goal:** By the end of this setup you will have a working free Canva account, know your way around the editor, and have a saved font pairing, a colour palette and a practice folder ready. You set things up by doing, not reading.

## What you'll be able to do
- Open Canva and create a blank design of any size
- Find templates, elements, text, uploads and the export button without searching
- Save a colour palette and a font pairing you can reuse
- Keep all your practice work in one tidy folder

## Before you start (about 10 minutes)
- Open [Canva](https://www.canva.com) in your browser. Canva works on a laptop, desktop or phone, but a bigger screen is easier for design.
- Warm-up: have one email you will use for this whole course. You will sign in with it every week.

## Try it 1 - Create your free Canva account
**Why it matters:** the free plan has everything you need for this course. You do not need Canva Pro.
**Do this now:**
1. Go to [canva.com](https://www.canva.com) and click **Sign up**.
2. Choose **Continue with Google** or **Sign up with email**. Pick the email you will keep using.
3. If Canva asks what you will use it for, choose **Personal** or **Student**. This only changes a few suggestions, nothing important.
4. When your home screen loads, you are in.
**You've got it when:** you can see the Canva home page with a search bar and a **Create a design** button.

## Try it 2 - Tour the editor
**Why it matters:** every task in this course happens inside the editor. Knowing where the five main tools live saves you hours.
**Do this now:**
1. Click **Create a design**, then search **Instagram Post (Square)** and pick the blank one.
2. On the left panel, click each tab in turn and notice what it does: **Design** (templates), **Elements** (shapes, lines, photos, icons), **Text** (headings and body text), **Uploads** (your own images), **Projects** (your saved work).
3. Click once on the blank white canvas, then click **Text** and **Add a heading**. Type your name.
4. Find the **Share** button at the top right. Click it, then **Download**. Do not download yet, just see that **PNG** and **JPG** options are there. Close the menu.
**You've got it when:** you can point to templates, elements, text, uploads and the download button from memory.

## Try it 3 - Set up your Brand Kit basics
**Why it matters:** a Brand Kit stores your colours and fonts so every design you make looks like it belongs together.
**Do this now:**
1. From the Canva home page, look on the left menu for **Brand**, then open **Brand Kit** (on free plans this may be called **Brand** with one kit).
2. Add a brand name, for example "My Practice Brand".
3. You will add colours and fonts in the next two steps. Leave this tab open.
**You've got it when:** you have an empty Brand Kit with a name, ready to fill.

## Try it 4 - Save a font pairing
**Why it matters:** picking two fonts that work together is half of good-looking design. Save one pair now and reuse it all course.
**Do this now:**
1. A safe, free pairing to start with: heading **Poppins** (or **Montserrat**), body **Open Sans** (or **Lato**). All four are free in Canva.
2. In your Brand Kit, under **Brand fonts**, set a heading font and a body font to that pair.
3. Make a quick test: on a blank design, add a heading in your heading font and a line of body text in your body font. Check they look clearly different but still friendly together.
**You've got it when:** your Brand Kit shows one heading font and one body font, and you have seen them side by side.

## Try it 5 - Save a 3 to 5 colour palette
**Why it matters:** a small, fixed set of colours stops your designs looking messy. You will reuse this palette every week.
**Do this now:**
1. Pick one main colour, one dark colour (for text), one light background colour, and one accent colour. For example: deep blue `#0B3B66`, near-black `#1A1A1A`, off-white `#F5F5F5`, warm accent `#C0392B`.
2. In your Brand Kit, under **Brand colours**, add each one by typing the hex code (the `#` value).
3. Keep it to 5 colours or fewer. More than that is hard to control.
**You've got it when:** your Brand Kit shows 3 to 5 saved colours you can click in any design.

## Try it 6 - Make a practice project folder
**Why it matters:** you will create many files. One folder keeps them findable instead of scattered.
**Do this now:**
1. On the Canva home page, open **Projects** on the left.
2. Click **Add new** (or the **+**), then **Folder**. Name it **Graphic Design Practice**.
3. Open the design you made in Try it 2, click the **...** menu, choose **Move to folder**, and put it in your new folder.
**You've got it when:** your practice folder exists and already holds at least one design.

## You are ready when
- [ ] You can sign in to your free Canva account
- [ ] You can open a blank design and find templates, elements, text, uploads and download
- [ ] Your Brand Kit has a name
- [ ] A heading font and a body font are saved
- [ ] A 3 to 5 colour palette is saved
- [ ] A **Graphic Design Practice** folder exists with one design in it
