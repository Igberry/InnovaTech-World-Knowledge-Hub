**Design work you are proud to show.** Graphic Design is a hands-on, 6-week course that takes you from zero to a
real portfolio using free Canva. You design something in every lesson, so you learn by making, not by reading
theory.

## Who this is for

Anyone who wants to create professional-looking graphics: small business owners, social media managers,
freelancers, and complete beginners. No design background or paid software needed, just a free Canva account and
a willingness to practise.

## What you'll be able to do

- Make any layout look cleaner with alignment, contrast, hierarchy, and whitespace
- Choose colours and fonts that look professional and read well
- Design social posts, stories, and carousels that match one brand
- Create a simple logo and a one-page brand kit
- Lay out flyers, posters, and a one-page document, and export print-ready PDFs
- Build a portfolio and a complete brand pack you can show clients

## The 6-week plan

| Week | Topic | Main deliverable |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Setup: Canva, brand kit, fonts and colours | A ready workspace and saved palette |
| 1 | Design principles | A bad flyer redesigned using all four principles |
| 2 | Colour and typography | A palette and font pairing, applied |
| 3 | Social media graphics | A 3-post set in one brand |
| 4 | Logos and brand kits | A wordmark logo and a one-page brand kit |
| 5 | Flyers, posters, documents | A flyer, a poster, and a print-ready PDF |
| 6 | Portfolio and capstone | A portfolio and a full client brand pack |

## Weekly time (about 6-8 hours)

| Activity | Hours |
|---|---|
| Follow the lesson and do the Try-it tasks | 3 |
| Design practice | 2 |
| Weekly assignment | 1.5 |
| In-person mentoring | 1.5 |
| **Total** | **~8** |

## How mentoring works

Each week has a 90-minute live group mentoring session: critique designs together, work through Canva live, and get
the brief for the next assignment. You do most of the designing on your own between sessions.

## Assessment and certification

| Component | Weight |
|---|---|
| Weekly assignments (Weeks 1-5) | 45% |
| Portfolio and capstone brand pack (Week 6) | 35% |
| Attendance and consistency | 20% |

A completion certificate is awarded at 70% or above overall.

## Honest outcomes

This course gives you real, practical design skills and a portfolio you can show. It does **not** guarantee a job,
clients, or income; that depends on your skills, effort, and the market. Canva's features and free tier change over
time, so the course teaches durable design judgement (principles, colour, type, layout) over any one tool's menus.

## Facilitator Notes

Beginners improve fastest when you critique real work against the four principles (Week 1) every session; make
critique a habit. Watch for over-decorated designs; push for clarity and whitespace. Keep logo work simple
(wordmarks beat clip-art icons for beginners). Because Canva changes, teach the judgement and have learners find
current features themselves.
