## Who this course is for

This beginner course is for job seekers, students, office staff, virtual assistants and small-business owners who want to work more confidently with everyday office documents and communication. It starts with Windows files and folders, then practises Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook, Teams and OneNote through a fictional small-business case: Yenagoa Fresh Bites.

The course teaches Microsoft 365 Apps as the main reference, while calling out meaningful differences in Office 2019/2021 and the free web apps. Current access and account requirements can vary by region and change; check the official Microsoft pages and your own account before planning around a feature. Learners without a licensed Microsoft desktop app can use the web versions where available or try LibreOffice for local document, spreadsheet and presentation practice. Some menus and advanced collaboration features will differ.

## Time budget

Plan for about six to eight hours of independent reading and practice each week, plus one 90-minute mentoring session. Actual time varies with device access, power and connectivity. Download the small practice files when connected, keep a local working copy, and sync only when the connection is stable. The Week 3 checkpoint is a separate 90-minute practical assessment.

## Assessment and completion

Weekly assignments from Weeks 1–5 contribute 40% in total. The Week 3 mid-course checkpoint contributes 15%; the Week 6 Office Admin Pack contributes 30%; attendance and consistent practice contribute 15%. The capstone rubric is accuracy 25%, professional design 25%, consistency 20%, appropriate tool choice 15% and organisation 15%.

The Office Admin Pack combines a business letter and mail merge, a budget and sales workbook, a ten-slide deck, a team meeting agenda and minutes, and a one-page office standards guide. Each item must be clearly labelled as a practice artifact and use only the fictional Fresh Bites case data.

## Tools and access

The course uses Windows, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook, Teams, OneNote and OneDrive. A laptop is strongly preferred for full document and spreadsheet tasks; a phone can review files and join an authorised practice meeting but is not a full replacement for desktop work. A Microsoft account may provide web and mobile app access, subject to the current regional offer and account requirements. Do not buy a subscription for this course unless you independently choose to and understand its renewal terms. Never use pirated software or share account passwords.

LibreOffice is a free, open-source desktop alternative for Writer, Calc and Impress. It is useful for practising transferable document, spreadsheet and slide skills, but some Microsoft-specific features and formatting may differ. Outlook, Teams, OneNote, OneDrive sharing, and Word mail merge may require different access or workflows in an alternative suite.

## Mentoring format

Each week includes a 90-minute guided session: review, a short feature demonstration, hands-on practice, learner explanation and assignment planning. Bring questions and a copy of your own work with personal details removed. If you have low data or unreliable power, download the week’s small files in advance and save locally as you work.

## Certification information

An optional Microsoft Office Specialist pathway exists for Microsoft 365 Apps. As checked on 12 November 2026, Microsoft Learn describes the Associate credential as requiring three qualifying exams in different programs, including at least one Microsoft 365 Apps exam; current exam details include Word MO-110, Excel MO-210 and PowerPoint MO-310. Microsoft says exam prices depend on the country or region where the exam is proctored. We have not verified a current Nigeria-specific price or test-centre schedule. This course is not exam preparation, does not include an exam fee, and does not guarantee certification, employment or income. Check the linked official Microsoft and Certiport information before deciding whether to register.

## Honest outcomes

Success means being able to organise files, produce clear office documents, use spreadsheet essentials, present information and handle routine communication with care. You will have a practice portfolio to discuss and improve. A portfolio can help show your skills, but it does not guarantee a job, client, income or certification result.
