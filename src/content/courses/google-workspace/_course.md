## Who this course is for

This beginner-friendly course is for virtual assistants, remote workers, students, teachers, NGO staff and small-business teams who want to organise work and collaborate clearly. It follows a fictional Yenagoa business, Fresh Bites, from inbox and calendar routines through documents, spreadsheets, presentations, forms, a simple site and a shared-team capstone.

The course uses a personal Google Account for learning where the feature is available. That is not the same as a paid Google Workspace business subscription. Organisation-managed accounts can have different menus, storage, sharing rules and administrator controls. Each optional paid-plan feature is labelled; check the current edition and your organisation's rules before planning around it. The course never asks the facilitator to create or change anything in a Google account.

## Time budget

Plan for about six to eight hours of independent reading and practice each week, plus one 90-minute mentoring session. Actual time varies with device access, power, connectivity and account features. Download the small starter files when connected, save working copies locally and sync when the connection is stable. A laptop is strongly preferred for Sheets, Slides, Sites and account-sharing exercises; a phone can review files and join an authorised meeting.

## Assessment and completion

Weekly assignments for Weeks 1–4 contribute 40% in total. The 90-minute Week 3 checkpoint contributes 15%; the Week 5 capstone contributes 30%; attendance and consistent practice contribute 15%. The capstone is assessed on workspace organisation and sharing (25%), correct use of tools (25%), collaboration design (20%), security habits (15%), and documentation and walkthrough (15%).

## Tools and access

The course practises Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Slides, Forms, Sites, Meet and Chat. Before starting, check which features are available in your account, device, browser and region. Some collaboration and administration features require an eligible Google Workspace edition or an administrator's permission. They are optional demonstrations, not prerequisites. Google's official plan comparison and help pages can change; links in the Resources page are starting points, not a promise that a feature is included in every account.

Keep the training workspace fictional. Do not upload student records, customer details, passwords, payment information or confidential work documents. Use a second account only if you control it or have explicit permission; remove practice sharing when the exercise ends. Never publish a form, Drive link or Google Site until you have checked what information it exposes and who can open it.

## Mentoring format

Each week includes a 90-minute session with a short review, demonstration, guided practice, learner explanation and assignment planning. Bring your own practice files with personal information removed. When power or data is unreliable, download the week’s small files in advance, enable offline access while connected if your browser and account support it, and save local copies. Offline editing does not guarantee that changes have synced; verify the file status after reconnecting.

## Optional certification pathway

As checked on 12 November 2026, Google Cloud lists the **Associate Google Workspace Administrator** certification. Its official page describes a two-hour exam, a $125 registration fee plus applicable tax, no formal prerequisites, and recommends six months of hands-on Super Admin experience in a real or test environment. This five-week beginner course is not exam preparation and does not provide an administrator environment. We have not verified Nigeria-specific exam pricing, currency, test-centre availability or remote-proctor eligibility. Check the official certification page before making a decision. A course portfolio is not a certification, and the course makes no promise of passing an exam, finding work or earning income.

## Honest outcomes

Success means organising a mailbox and calendar, producing clear documents and summaries, building a spreadsheet dashboard, creating a useful form and presentation, and sharing files with care. You will have fictional practice artifacts and a walkthrough to improve. These demonstrate practice; they do not guarantee a job, client, income or certification result.
