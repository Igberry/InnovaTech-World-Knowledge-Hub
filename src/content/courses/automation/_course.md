**Stop doing the same boring task over and over.** Automation means setting things up so repetitive work
happens on its own, freeing your time for the work that actually needs a human. This 8-week course is
practical and **no-code**: you will automate real tasks using free tools, without programming. It follows
on naturally from the [Artificial Intelligence](/courses/artificial-intelligence/) course, where you
documented workflows; here you make them run.

## Who this is for

Anyone who wants to save time and work smarter, for a job, a business, freelancing, or study. You need
basic computer and internet skills and a willingness to test carefully. Every tool used has a free way to
start. It pairs well with the AI, Virtual Assistant, and Data Analysis courses.

## What you'll be able to do

- Find the tasks worth automating (and recognise the ones to leave alone)
- Win back time with built-in automations you already have
- Use spreadsheets to remove repetitive manual work
- Connect apps so that "when X happens, Y happens" automatically
- Build multi-step automations with conditions, and test them safely
- Keep automations reliable, private, and under human control
- Automate a real process from start to finish

## How mentoring works

Each week has a 90-minute in-person mentoring session: review your work, build and test automations live,
and get the brief for the next assignment. You do most of the work on your own between sessions.

## Assessment and certification

| Component | Weight |

A completion certificate is awarded at 70% or above overall.

## A note on safety

Automation can repeat a mistake very fast, so this course treats testing, rollback plans, and keeping a
human in charge of risky decisions as core skills, not afterthoughts. You will never be asked to point a
new automation at real customers, real money, or the only copy of important data without testing first.

## Honest outcomes

This course gives you real, practical automation skills and the judgement to use them safely. It does
**not** guarantee a job or income; that depends on your skills, effort, and the market. Tools and free
tiers also change, so the course teaches durable thinking (spotting tasks, triggers and actions, testing)
over any one tool's buttons.

## Facilitator Notes

The two habits that matter most are choosing the right tasks (Week 1) and testing with a rollback plan
(Weeks 5 and 7). Spend extra time there; a careless automation does damage quickly. Build and test live in
sessions. Keep reinforcing privacy (do not route sensitive data through third-party tools carelessly) and
platform rules (no spam or scraping). Because tools change, teach the trigger-action thinking, not one
tool's menus, and have learners verify current free tiers themselves.
