**AI is now a workplace skill, like email or spreadsheets.** The people who get ahead are not the ones
who fear it or blindly trust it, but the ones who use it well: to work faster, learn quicker, and create
more, while staying firmly in charge of accuracy and judgement. This 8-week course makes you one of those
people. It is practical and tool-focused, not a maths or machine-learning course.

## Who this is for

Anyone who wants to use AI to do real work better, whether for a job, a business, studies, or freelancing.
You need basic computer and internet skills and a willingness to try things and check your results. Every
tool used has a free way to start. This course pairs well with every other InnovaTech course: AI is a
multiplier on skills you already have.

## What you'll be able to do

- Explain what AI really is (and is not) in plain language
- Write prompts that get genuinely useful results
- Use AI to draft, edit, summarise, translate, research, and create images
- Catch AI's mistakes, bias, and made-up facts before they cause harm
- Protect privacy and respect copyright when using AI
- Build and document a simple AI-assisted workflow
- Finish a real AI-powered project for your portfolio

## How mentoring works

Each week has a 90-minute in-person mentoring session: review your work, practise live (prompting drills,
fact-check challenges, workflow design), and get the brief for the next assignment. You do most of the work
on your own between sessions.

## Assessment and certification

| Component | Weight |
|---|---|
| Weekly assignments (Weeks 1-7) | 45% |
| Capstone project and presentation (Week 8) | 35% |
| Attendance and consistency | 20% |

A completion certificate is awarded at 70% or above overall.

## A note on responsible use

This course treats AI as a powerful assistant you supervise, never as an unchecked replacement for your own
judgement. You will practise checking facts, protecting privacy, respecting copyright, and being honest about
where you use AI. These habits are part of the skill, not an afterthought.

## Honest outcomes

This course gives you real, current AI skills and the judgement to use them well. It does **not** guarantee a
job or income; that depends on your skills, effort, and the market. AI tools also change quickly, so this
course teaches durable habits (clear prompting, verifying, responsible use) over memorising any one tool.

## Facilitator Notes

The two habits that matter most are iterating on prompts (Week 2) and fact-checking output (Weeks 5 and 7) -
spend extra time there, because they separate skilled users from naive ones. Run live prompting and
fact-check drills. Reinforce privacy (never paste sensitive data) and honesty (no passing AI work off
dishonestly) every week. Because tools change fast, teach the thinking, not the clicks, and have learners
verify current free tiers themselves. Keep examples local and realistic.
