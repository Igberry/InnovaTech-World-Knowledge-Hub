**Goal:** Find real remote and freelance openings, apply with tailored messages, track your progress,
and - crucially - recognise and avoid the scams that target new remote workers.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this week you can:

- Search the main platforms and boards for real openings in your field
- Write tailored applications and short outreach messages
- Track applications and follow up professionally
- Identify common scams and protect yourself

## Pre-Session Resources (about 2 hours)

- Download the **application tracker** and **outreach templates** (files box above).
- Browse (don't pay for anything yet): **linkedin.com/jobs**, and search for well-known remote job
  boards and freelance platforms in your field.
- Watch (search YouTube): *"how to avoid online job scams"* from a reputable source.

## Lesson 1: Where remote work lives

| Channel | Good for | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **LinkedIn Jobs** + search | Employed roles | Use filters: Remote, your role |
| **Remote job boards** | Remote-first companies | Search "remote job board [your field]" |
| **Freelance platforms** | Short projects, building a track record | e.g. Upwork, Fiverr; read current fees/terms |
| **Referrals & networking** | Often the best when starting | Tell people what you're looking for |
| **Direct outreach** | Small businesses needing help | Use the outreach templates, personalise each |

Don't rely on one channel. Mix job boards, a freelance platform, and direct outreach.

## Lesson 2: Applying well

- **Tailor** every application (Week 2): match the advert's words, lead with your most relevant point.
- Keep cover notes **short and specific** (use the templates, change the first line every time).
- Apply to roles you're a **reasonable** fit for - you don't need 100% of the requirements.
- **Volume + quality:** many applications, each tailored. Most people hear back from a fraction.

> **Rule of thumb:** If an application takes more than ~15 minutes, you're overthinking it. Tailor the
> key points and move on. Consistency across many applications beats perfection on one.

## Lesson 3: Track and follow up

Open the **application tracker** (CSV) and log every application: date, company, role, source, link,
status, and a follow-up date. Follow up politely after ~5-7 days if you haven't heard back (template 4
in the outreach file). Tracking keeps you organised and shows you patterns (which sources work).

## Lesson 4: Avoiding scams (read carefully)

New remote workers are actively targeted. Protect yourself:

> **Warning: never pay to get a job.** Legitimate employers and clients do **not** charge you a "fee",
> "training cost", "equipment deposit", or "registration" to start work. If they ask you to pay, it's a
> scam.

Other red flags:

- **Overpayment / fake cheque:** a "client" overpays and asks you to refund the difference. The original
  payment is fake; you lose your own money. Never refund from your own funds.
- **"Trial tasks" that are real work:** a long, unpaid "test" that is actually a finished deliverable
  for their business. A tiny, clearly-scoped sample is fine; building their real project free is not.
- **Too good to be true pay** for little work, or urgent pressure to act fast.
- **Vague company, no real online presence,** or contact only via a personal chat app.
- **Requests for sensitive data** (bank PIN, BVN, passwords) or to move to crypto immediately.

**Protect yourself:** verify the company (website, LinkedIn, reviews); use the platform's payment
protection; get a deposit for direct clients; never share passwords or banking secrets; trust your gut -
if it feels off, walk away.

## Common mistakes

- Applying to one job a week and expecting results.
- The same untailored message to everyone.
- Not tracking applications, so follow-ups never happen.
- Ignoring red flags because the offer is exciting.

## Practice Exercises

1. **Easy:** Find and save 10 real openings that fit your goal.
2. **Easy:** Set up the application tracker with your first entries.
3. **Medium:** Write a tailored application for three of them.
4. **Medium:** Write two personalised outreach messages to small businesses.
5. **Medium:** List five scam red flags in your own words.
6. **Stretch:** Research one company thoroughly (site, LinkedIn, reviews) and note what you found.
7. **Stretch:** Draft a polite follow-up for an application you sent.

## Weekly Assignment (Due Before Week 5)

Run a real, tracked **mini job search**:

1. **10+ openings** saved, matched to your goal.
2. **5 tailored applications** sent (job boards, platforms, or direct outreach).
3. Everything logged in the **application tracker**, with follow-up dates.
4. A short **"scam red flags" note** (5+ flags and how you'd respond).

## Facilitator Notes

Make the scam section vivid and non-negotiable - new remote workers in Nigeria are heavily targeted,
and the "pay to get a job" and overpayment scams cause real losses. Push volume with quality: learners
who send one application a week stall. Check that outreach messages are personalised, not blasted. Keep
learners' application details private.
