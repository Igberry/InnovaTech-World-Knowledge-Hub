**Remote work opens doors far beyond Yenagoa.** This 6-week course gets you ready to compete for
remote and freelance roles: a credible professional brand, proof of your skills, a smart job search,
and the confidence to interview and get hired. It pairs well with any InnovaTech skills course - those
teach the skill; this one helps you get the work.

## Who this is for

Anyone preparing for remote or freelance work - whether you're taking another InnovaTech course (Data
Analysis, Web Development) or already have a skill to offer. You need basic computer and internet
skills and a willingness to practise. Every tool used is free.

## What you'll be able to do

- Write a one-page, remote-ready CV and a complete LinkedIn profile
- Show proof of your work with a simple portfolio and references
- Find real openings on the right platforms and apply with tailored messages
- Recognise and avoid the scams that target new remote workers
- Communicate well in writing and async, use common remote tools, and work reliably on unreliable power and data
- Prepare for and handle remote interviews, and understand how to get paid

## The 6-week plan

| Week | Topic | Main deliverable |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Setup: accounts, tools, workspace | Accounts created; a reliable workspace + power/data plan |
| 1 | What remote work really is | A clear, realistic picture of remote work and your goal |
| 2 | Your professional brand | A remote-ready CV + polished LinkedIn profile |
| 3 | Proof of work and portfolio | A simple personal profile/site + references (with permission) |
| 4 | Finding remote work (and avoiding scams) | A shortlist of openings + tailored applications sent |
| 5 | Remote work skills | Async communication, the common tools, time-zone and reliability habits |
| 6 | Interviews and getting hired | A mock interview + a complete application package |

## Weekly time (about 7 hours)

| Activity | Hours |
|---|---|
| Read the lesson and do the exercises | 3 |
| Weekly assignment | 2 |
| In-person mentoring | 1.5 |
| **Total** | **~7** |

## How mentoring works

Each week has a 90-minute in-person mentoring session: review, practice (mock interviews, profile
reviews, live application writing), and a brief for the next assignment. You do most of the work on
your own between sessions.

## Assessment and certification

| Component | Weight |
|---|---|
| Weekly assignments (Weeks 1-5) | 45% |
| Final package and mock interview (Week 6) | 35% |
| Attendance and consistency | 20% |

A completion certificate is awarded at 70% or above overall.

## Honest outcomes

This course makes you genuinely **remote-ready** - a credible profile, a portfolio, a working job
search, and interview practice. It does **not** guarantee a job or any income; that depends on your
skills, your effort, and the market. Success here means a complete application package and consistent,
confident applying.

## Facilitator Notes

The biggest wins are a real CV and LinkedIn profile (Weeks 2-3) and honest scam-awareness (Week 4) -
spend extra time there. Run live mock interviews in Week 6; recording them (with permission) helps
learners see their own progress. Keep every learner's details private; never post names or photos
without written consent.
