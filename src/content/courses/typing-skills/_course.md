## Who this course is for

This four-week course is for beginners who currently type with one or two fingers, look down at the keyboard, or want more confidence with work documents and data entry. It is useful preparation for office, transcription, virtual-assistant and remote-work tasks. You do not need a particular starting speed. A computer with a physical keyboard is recommended; the phone-typing section is supplementary, not a substitute for keyboard practice.

By the end, you will practise touch typing with the home row, use all letters, capitals, numbers and punctuation, enter data carefully, use common keyboard shortcuts and assemble a small portfolio of realistic typing tasks.

## Weekly plan

| Week | Focus | Main deliverable |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Posture, home row and first letters | Baseline test and five-day practice log |
| 2 | Full alphabet, capitals and accuracy | Accuracy test, weak-key report and practice log |
| 3 | Numbers, punctuation, numpad and shortcuts | 100-row data-entry task and shortcuts quiz |
| 4 | Speed building and real-world typing | Final tests and work-typing portfolio |

## Time budget

Plan for 30 minutes of practice on five days each week, plus one 60-minute weekly mentoring session. That is about 3.5 hours per week or 14 hours over the course. Short, consistent practice is more useful than one long session. If power or internet is unreliable, use the printable exercises and offline documents in the downloads.

## Tools and accounts

You need a computer keyboard, a word processor or text editor, a spreadsheet for Week 3, and a way to record timed tests. You may use the local [Typing Practice tool](/tools/typing-practice/) built for this course. It runs without a typing-service account and saves only test summaries in your browser's local storage.

As checked on 12 November 2026, TypingClub says its individual lessons are free and do not require an account, although an optional profile saves progress. 10FastFingers offers a free one-minute typing test and text-practice modes. Features and terms can change; confirm them on each official site before relying on them:

- [TypingClub](https://www.typingclub.com/)
- [10FastFingers](https://10fastfingers.com/)

Do not enter personal information into a practice site unless you understand why it is requested. An account is not needed for this course.

## Running practice case

We use **Yenagoa Fresh Bites**, a fictional restaurant and takeaway in Yenagoa, Bayelsa State. It has six fictional staff members and receives orders by WhatsApp, phone and walk-in. All names, prices, phone numbers and sample records in the course are invented for learning. Do not copy real customer information into your practice files.

## Weekly time and assessment

Your assessment is based on consistent practice and accuracy, not comparison with other learners:

| Component | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Daily practice log | 30% |
| Weekly timed tests and progress | 25% |
| Keyboard-shortcuts quiz | 10% |
| Work-typing tasks | 20% |
| Final 1-, 3- and 5-minute tests | 15% |

The suggested adjustable beginner goal is **35 WPM at 95% accuracy** by the end of Week 4. A certificate requires at least 70% overall and a recorded final test at the learner's personal target or at least **30 WPM at 95% accuracy**, whichever is higher. Your facilitator can agree a different realistic personal target based on your baseline and access needs.

**WPM** is calculated as correct characters divided by five, then divided by elapsed minutes. **Accuracy** is correct characters divided by all typed characters, multiplied by 100. The local practice tool uses these formulas; other sites may calculate results differently.

## Mentoring

Each week's 60-minute session includes a posture and hand check, review of the practice log, coaching on the week's keys, a timed test, a fix plan for weak keys and a specific practice goal. Sessions are not a race. Feedback focuses on relaxed technique, accuracy and steady improvement.

## Is this right for me?

This course is a good fit if you can use a computer keyboard and are willing to practise regularly. It is also suitable if your speed is already reasonable but accuracy, numbers or work documents need attention. If typing causes pain or persistent discomfort, stop, adjust your position and seek appropriate professional advice; this course is not medical treatment.

Success means building a dependable skill, keeping evidence of your practice and using it consistently. It does not guarantee a job or income. Ask on WhatsApp about enrolment; **no course fees are published here**.
