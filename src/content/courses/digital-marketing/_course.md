**Market online, end to end.** Digital Marketing is a practical, 8-week course that walks the whole journey, from
a stranger's first click to a customer, across search, content, email, and ads. You build real assets each week
with free tools, and finish by running a small campaign from start to finish.

## Who this is for

Anyone who wants to bring in customers online: business owners, freelancers, and anyone aiming for a digital
marketing role. You need basic computer and internet skills. No prior marketing experience needed. It pairs well
with the Social Media Marketing and Graphic Design courses.

## What you'll be able to do

- See how strangers become customers, and fix where a funnel leaks
- Build a clear landing page with a single offer and a strong call to action
- Do keyword research and apply on-page SEO so pages get found
- Plan helpful content and turn one piece into social and email
- Write a welcome email and a short nurture sequence
- Set up a basic ad in draft, with an audience, budget, and objective
- Read the core Google Analytics 4 reports and decide what to change

## The 8-week plan

| Week | Topic | Main deliverable |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Setup: Google, GA4, email tool, Canva, offer sheet | Accounts ready and a one-page offer sheet |
| 1 | The customer journey and funnels | A mapped journey and funnel for one product |
| 2 | Landing pages that convert | A live, simple landing page |
| 3 | SEO basics | A page optimised, plus a Google Business Profile |
| 4 | Content marketing | A content plan and one piece repurposed |
| 5 | Email marketing | A welcome email and a 3-email sequence |
| 6 | Paid ads | A complete ad set up in draft |
| 7 | Analytics (GA4) | A short report and one data-based decision |
| 8 | Capstone: a mini campaign | A campaign run and presented end to end |

## Weekly time (about 6-8 hours)

| Activity | Hours |
|---|---|
| Follow the lesson and do the Try-it tasks | 3 |
| Build assets / practice | 2 |
| Weekly assignment | 1.5 |
| In-person mentoring | 1.5 |
| **Total** | **~8** |

## How mentoring works

Each week has a 90-minute live group mentoring session: review your work, build assets together, and get the brief
for the next assignment. You do most of the work on your own between sessions.

## Assessment and certification

| Component | Weight |
|---|---|
| Weekly assignments (Weeks 1-7) | 45% |
| Capstone campaign and presentation (Week 8) | 35% |
| Attendance and consistency | 20% |

A completion certificate is awarded at 70% or above overall.

## Honest outcomes

This course gives you real, practical digital marketing skills and a set of assets you can show. It does **not**
guarantee a job or income; that depends on your skills, effort, and the market. Tools and free tiers change, so the
course teaches durable thinking (funnels, offers, measurement) over any one platform's buttons. You will never be
asked to spend real ad money carelessly; ads are set up and reviewed in draft first.

## Facilitator Notes

The highest-leverage weeks are the funnel (Week 1), the landing page (Week 2), and measurement (Week 7); if time is
short, protect those. Keep ads in draft until learners understand cost-per-result, so nobody burns a budget. Stress
permission and anti-spam rules for email. Because tools change, teach the concepts and have learners verify current
free tiers themselves.
