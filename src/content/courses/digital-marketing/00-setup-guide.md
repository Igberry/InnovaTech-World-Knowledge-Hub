**Goal:** In one short session you will create the free accounts this course runs on, open each one at least once, and write a one-page offer sheet for the product or service you will market for the next 8 weeks. You set everything up by doing, not reading.

## What you'll be able to do

- Log into a free Google account and a free Google Analytics 4 property
- Log into a free email-marketing account and a free Canva account
- Describe, in one page, what you sell, who it is for, and the main benefit

## Before you start (about 10 minutes)

- Open a blank document (Google Docs or a Word file) and title it "My Offer Sheet".
- Warm-up: in one sentence, write down the product, service, or idea you want to market. A real one is best. If you have none, pick something you know well, for example a friend's catering business or a tutoring side-gig.

## Try it 1 - Create or confirm a free Google account

**Why it matters:** your Google account unlocks Analytics, Business Profile, and most of the free tools in this course. One account covers all of them.

**Do this now:**
1. Go to https://accounts.google.com and sign in, or click "Create account" and choose "For my personal use".
2. Pick a professional-looking address, for example yourname.marketing@gmail.com, not a nickname.
3. Turn on 2-Step Verification under Security so the account stays safe.

**You've got it when:** you can open https://mail.google.com and see your inbox while signed in.

## Try it 2 - Set up a free Google Analytics 4 property

**Why it matters:** Analytics shows you who visits, where they came from, and what they do. You will use it in Week 7. Setting it up now means data starts collecting early.

**Do this now:**
1. Go to https://analytics.google.com and click "Start measuring".
2. Create an account name (your business or your own name) and a property named after your offer.
3. If you do not have a website yet, do not worry. Open the official GA4 Demo Account at https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/6367342 and click the link to add it, so you have live data to practise on.

**You've got it when:** you can open a report in Analytics and see at least one number, either from your own property or the demo account.

## Try it 3 - Create a free email-marketing account

**Why it matters:** email is the one channel you own. A platform like Brevo or Mailchimp lets you collect addresses and send proper campaigns for free at small sizes. You use it in Week 5.

**Do this now:**
1. Choose one tool and sign up free: Brevo at https://www.brevo.com or Mailchimp at https://www.mailchimp.com.
2. Complete the basic profile (your name, your "from" name, your country).
3. Find the "Contacts" or "Audience" area and the "Campaigns" area, and click into each once so you know where they live.

**You've got it when:** you are logged into one email tool and can find where contacts are stored and where campaigns are created.

## Try it 4 - Create a free Canva account

**Why it matters:** Canva is how you will make landing pages, social posts, and email images without a designer. It is free and runs in your browser.

**Do this now:**
1. Go to https://www.canva.com and sign up with your new Google account (fastest route).
2. In the search bar, type "Instagram post" and open one blank template so you see the editor.
3. Change one word of text and one colour, just to prove you can edit. Then close it.

**You've got it when:** you have opened the Canva editor and successfully changed text and a colour on a design.

## Try it 5 - Write your one-page offer sheet

**Why it matters:** every lesson in this course points back to one offer. A clear offer sheet keeps all 8 weeks focused on the same real thing.

**Do this now, in your "My Offer Sheet" document, answer these in a line or two each:**
1. **What you sell:** the product or service in plain words.
2. **Who it is for:** the one main type of customer (for example, "busy parents in Lagos who want home-cooked meals delivered").
3. **The main benefit:** the single biggest reason someone buys, finished from their point of view, for example "saves me two hours of cooking every evening".
4. **The price range:** roughly what it costs.
5. **The action you want:** what a visitor should do next (call, order, book, sign up).

**You've got it when:** your offer sheet fits on one page and a friend could read it and say back what you sell and why it is worth buying.

### Watch out for

- Signing up for several email tools at once. Pick one and learn it well.
- Skipping the offer sheet because it feels like "just writing". It is the backbone of every later week.
- Using a joke email address. Your "from" name shows up in real inboxes.

## You are ready when

- [ ] You can sign into a free Google account with 2-Step Verification on
- [ ] A Google Analytics 4 property (your own or the demo) shows live numbers
- [ ] You are logged into one free email tool and can find contacts and campaigns
- [ ] You have opened the Canva editor and edited a design
- [ ] Your one-page offer sheet names what you sell, who for, the main benefit, price, and the next action

2. Where would you go to create an email campaign in the tool you chose?
