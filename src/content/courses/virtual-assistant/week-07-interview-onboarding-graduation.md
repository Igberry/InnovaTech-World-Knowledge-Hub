**Goal:** Start a client relationship professionally, think beyond client #1, and graduate with a career
package.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this week you can:

- Onboard a first client cleanly
- Send proactive, professional check-in messages
- Build communication habits that lead to retainers
- Diversify beyond one client and set a 90-day plan
- Assemble and present your complete VA Career Package

## How This Week Runs

| Session | Day | Length | What you bring |
|---|---|---|---|
| Session A (Teaching) | Day 1 | 60 min | Your onboarding questions |
| Quick Reference note | Day 2 | 10 to 15 min read | Nothing, just read it |
| Session B (Teaching + Practice) | Day 3 | 75 min | Your current application status and goals |
| Session C (Graduation, in person) | Day 5 | 90 to 120 min | Your career package; current application status |

## Session A (Teaching, Day 1): Onboarding Your First Client the Right Way

- **First-week checklist:** access setup (the safe way), communication expectations, and the first
  deliverable (see the [onboarding checklist](/downloads/virtual-assistant/client-onboarding-checklist.md)).
- **Ask clarifying questions early** instead of guessing, it builds trust and prevents rework.
- **Set a weekly check-in rhythm** from day one.

*Learner action:* draft your personal **client-onboarding checklist**.

## Quick Reference (Day 2): Client Check-In Message Examples

Proactive updates keep clients (see the [examples](/downloads/virtual-assistant/client-check-in-message-examples.md)):

- A **start-of-week plan**
- A **mid-week update**
- An **end-of-week summary**
- An **"I noticed a problem"** message (flag early, with a suggested fix)

Predictable, results-led updates mean a client rarely needs to chase you, which is exactly why they keep
you.

## Session B (Teaching + Practice, Day 3): Communication Habits & a Sustainable Career

- **Over-communicate in the first month**, it builds trust fast.
- **Ask for feedback and testimonials** (there are gentle scripts in the examples download).
- **Diversify income** across 2 to 3 clients so you're not dependent on one.
- **Keep learning** to raise your rates over time (other InnovaTech courses help here).
- **Set a 90-day goal** (see the [template](/downloads/virtual-assistant/ninety-day-goal-template.md)).

*Learner action:* set a personal **90-day client/income goal**.

## Session C (Graduation, in person, Day 5): Final Mock Interviews & Graduation

- One **final mock interview** if you don't yet have a confirmed client or interview.
- A **personal recap** of your 7-week journey.
- Your **Certificate of Completion** presented 1:1 (awarded at 70%+ overall).
- **Optional introductions** to the mentor's network for referrals.

*Learner action:* attend in person if possible; bring your current application status for a final 1:1
review.

## This Week's Job-Hunt Focus

**Close out.** Either **confirm a first client/job**, or leave with a **fully active pipeline** and a
**concrete next-2-weeks plan**. **Success is measured by momentum**, not a guaranteed job on day 49.

## Final Deliverable: The VA Career Package

Assemble everything (use the [career package checklist](/downloads/virtual-assistant/va-career-package-checklist.md)):

1. **Niche Statement** (Week 1)
2. **Three live profiles** (Week 2)
3. **Portfolio + Rate Sheet** (Week 4)
4. **Full Application Tracker log** (Weeks 3 to 6)
5. Either a **signed client/job** or a **documented next-steps plan** (your 90-day goal)

### Grading guide

| Criteria | Weight |
|---|---|
| Complete, polished career package | 40% |
| Onboarding checklist + check-in habits ready | 20% |
| 90-day plan is specific and realistic | 20% |
| Final outcome: signed client/job OR a clear dated plan | 20% |

### Definition of done

- [ ] All five career-package items assembled
- [ ] Onboarding checklist and check-in examples ready to use
- [ ] 90-day goal set
- [ ] A confirmed client/interview or a dated next-2-weeks plan

## Self-Check Questions

1. What must be agreed in writing before you start work for a client?
2. What proactive check-ins keep a client happy, and how often?
3. Why diversify beyond one client, and how will you start?
4. What does "success is measured by momentum" mean for your graduation?

## Facilitator Notes

**Prepare:** the certificate template (blue and white branded, **do not use gold**), a simple graduation
venue (a cafe or office is fine), optional introduction/referral contacts, and a final mock-interview slot
if a learner needs it. Make graduation a genuine milestone: recap each learner's progress, present the
certificate personally, and set them up with momentum, an active pipeline and a 90-day plan. For learners
without a client yet, keep outcomes honest and encouraging: a complete package plus consistent applying is
success. Reinforce diversifying beyond one client and continued learning.
