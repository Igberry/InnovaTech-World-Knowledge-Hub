**Staying safe online is a skill everyone needs, and one that opens doors in a growing field.** This 8-week
course is practical and **defensive**: it teaches you how to protect your accounts, devices, data, and a
small organisation, and how to recover calmly when something goes wrong. It does **not** teach how to attack
anyone; it teaches how to keep yourself and others safe, legally and ethically.

## Who this is for

Anyone who uses the internet and wants to be safer, and anyone curious about cybersecurity as a field. You
need basic computer and internet skills. Every tool used has a free way to start. It pairs especially well
with the [Remote Ready](/courses/remote-ready/), [Virtual Assistant](/courses/virtual-assistant/),
[Artificial Intelligence](/courses/artificial-intelligence/), and [Automation](/courses/automation/)
courses, all of which involve handling accounts and data safely.

## What you'll be able to do

- Understand the real threats and judge your own risk honestly
- Lock down your accounts with strong passwords, 2FA, and safe recovery
- Recognise and avoid phishing, scams, and manipulation
- Keep your devices updated and your data backed up and recoverable
- Browse, use networks, and share online more privately
- Help a small business protect its data with simple, sensible rules
- Know exactly what to do if an account is hacked or a device is lost

## How mentoring works

Each week has a 90-minute in-person mentoring session: review your work, practise live (spotting phishing,
auditing accounts, planning recovery), and get the brief for the next assignment. You do most of the work on
your own between sessions.

## Assessment and certification

| Component | Weight |
|---|---|
| Weekly assignments (Weeks 1-7) | 45% |
| Capstone security plan and presentation (Week 8) | 35% |
| Attendance and consistency | 20% |

A completion certificate is awarded at 70% or above overall.

## Ethics first (please read)

This course is strictly about **defence and safety**. You will only ever practise on your **own** accounts,
devices, and data, or on an organisation's **with clear permission**. Accessing, testing, or interfering
with systems, accounts, or data that are not yours is wrong and usually illegal, and this course will never
teach or condone it. Good security people are trusted precisely because they respect that line.

## Honest outcomes

This course makes you genuinely safer online and gives you a solid, practical foundation. It does **not**
make you or any system "unhackable" (nothing is), and it does **not** guarantee a job or income in
cybersecurity; that depends on further study, effort, and the market. Security is an ongoing habit, not a
one-time fix.

## Facilitator Notes

The highest-value weeks are account security (Week 2), phishing (Week 3), and incident response (Week 7) -
these prevent and limit the most common real harm. Keep everything hands-on and local (mobile-money scams,
WhatsApp takeovers, SIM issues are very relevant). Reinforce the ethics line in every session: defence only,
own systems only, permission always. Make reporting mistakes blame-free. Never present or demonstrate actual
attack techniques against others; keep the focus on protection and recovery.
