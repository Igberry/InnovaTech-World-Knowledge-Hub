# LinkedIn profile checklist (remote-ready)

Tick each item. A complete, clear profile is often the first thing a remote employer sees.

## The basics
- [ ] Clear, friendly **profile photo** (plain background, your face, good light)
- [ ] **Headline** states your role + skills, not "unemployed" (e.g. "Virtual Assistant | Inbox & Calendar | Remote")
- [ ] **Location** set to your city (you can note "Open to remote")
- [ ] Custom profile **URL** (edit it to your name)
- [ ] Professional **email** connected

## About section
- [ ] 3-5 short paragraphs: who you are, what you do, what you're looking for
- [ ] First person, warm and specific; no jargon dump
- [ ] Ends with how to contact you

## Experience & skills
- [ ] Each role has 2-4 bullet achievements (action verb + result)
- [ ] 5-10 relevant **skills** listed
- [ ] Any InnovaTech course added under "Licenses & certifications" or "Education" once completed
- [ ] Projects or a portfolio link added

## Proof & activity
- [ ] 1-3 **recommendations** requested from people who know your work (with their permission)
- [ ] "Open to work" set for the role types you want (you can make it visible to recruiters only)
- [ ] Follow a few companies/people in your target field; comment thoughtfully now and then

## Final check
- [ ] Read it aloud - does it sound like a real person who can help a team?
- [ ] No spelling errors (paste into a spell-checker)
- [ ] Nothing private (home address, ID numbers) shown
