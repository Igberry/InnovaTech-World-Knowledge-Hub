### Tips
- Lead bullets with action verbs (Managed, Built, Reduced, Organised).
- Use numbers where possible ("20 clients", "3 hours saved per week").
- Match keywords from the job advert.
- No photo, age, marital status, or ID numbers needed for most remote roles.
