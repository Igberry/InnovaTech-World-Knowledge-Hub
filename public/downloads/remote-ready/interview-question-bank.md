# Remote interview question bank

Practice answering these out loud, then written. Use the **STAR** method for experience questions:
**S**ituation, **T**ask, **A**ction, **R**esult.

## About you
- Tell me about yourself. (60-90 seconds, role-focused, not your life story.)
- Why do you want this role / to work remotely?
- What are your strengths? One weakness and how you manage it?

## Remote-specific
- How do you stay organised and productive working from home?
- How do you handle communication across time zones?
- Your internet or power goes out during work hours - what's your plan?
- What tools have you used for remote work (chat, video, task boards)?
- How do you ask for help when your team is async and offline?

## Experience (answer with STAR)
- Tell me about a time you solved a problem on your own.
- Describe a time you missed a deadline or made a mistake. What happened next?
- Give an example of handling difficult feedback or a difficult client.

## Role-specific (adapt to your field)
- [A task they'd expect you to do day one] - walk me through how you'd do it.
- What would you do in your first week if hired?

## Your questions for them (always ask 2-3)
- What does a typical week look like for this role?
- How does the team communicate and stay in sync?
- What would success look like in the first 3 months?
- What are the working hours / expected overlap?

### Reminders
- It's fine to say "I haven't used that, but here's how I'd learn it."
- Have your CV, a glass of water, and a quiet, well-lit spot ready.
- Test your camera, mic and internet before the call.
