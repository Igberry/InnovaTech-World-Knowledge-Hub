# Outreach & application message templates

Keep messages short, specific, and about **them**, not you. Replace [brackets]. Never mass-send the
same text - change at least the first line for each person. All examples are fictional.

## 1. LinkedIn connection note (300 characters max)
> Hi [Name], I follow [Company]'s work in [area]. I'm a [your role] focused on [skill]. Would love to
> connect and learn from your team's approach.

## 2. Short application note (with a CV)
> Hi [Name], I'm applying for the [role] position. I [one concrete, relevant thing you've done that
> matches the advert]. My CV is attached, and you can see examples of my work here: [link]. I'm
> available [your hours / overlap with their time zone]. Thank you for considering me. - [Your name]

## 3. Cold outreach to a small business (freelance)
> Hi [Name], I came across [their business] and noticed [a specific, genuine observation]. I help
> [who] with [what you do], and I think I could help with [specific outcome]. If useful, I'd be glad
> to share a couple of ideas - no obligation. - [Your name], [link]

## 4. Follow-up (after no reply, wait ~5-7 days)
> Hi [Name], just following up on my note about the [role]. I'm still very interested and happy to do
> a short task to show how I'd help. Thanks for your time. - [Your name]

## 5. After an interview (same day)
> Hi [Name], thank you for the conversation today about [role]. I enjoyed hearing about [specific
> thing]. It confirmed I'd love to contribute to [goal]. Please let me know if you need anything else
> from me. - [Your name]

### Rules
- Proofread every message. One typo can cost you the reply.
- Be honest about your experience. Never promise what you can't do.
- Keep a record in your application tracker.
