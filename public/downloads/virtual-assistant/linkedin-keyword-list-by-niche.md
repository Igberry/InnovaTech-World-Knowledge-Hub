# LinkedIn Keywords Recruiters Search For (by Niche)

> Recruiters and clients search LinkedIn and job platforms by keywords. Use the ones that fit your niche
> naturally in your headline, About section, and skills, do not stuff them awkwardly. These are starting
> lists; add the exact words you see in real job posts for your niche.

## General / admin VA

virtual assistant, administrative assistant, inbox management, email management, calendar management,
scheduling, data entry, file organization, travel coordination, executive support

## Executive assistant

executive assistant, executive support, calendar management, inbox triage, meeting coordination, travel
management, gatekeeping, project coordination

## Social media VA

social media assistant, social media management, content scheduling, Canva, caption writing, community
management, Instagram, Facebook, content calendar, engagement

## E-commerce VA

e-commerce assistant, order processing, product listing, Shopify, customer support, returns, inventory
updates, order fulfillment

## Customer service VA

customer service, customer support, help desk, live chat, ticketing, CRM, FAQ, email support, complaint
resolution

## Bookkeeping-lite VA

bookkeeping assistant, expense tracking, invoicing, receipts, data entry, spreadsheets, accounts admin

## Real estate VA

real estate assistant, listing coordination, CRM updates, appointment setting, lead follow-up, transaction
coordination

## How to use them

- Put your **1 to 3 most important** keywords in your **headline**.
- Weave others **naturally** into your About section and experience.
- Add the relevant ones to your **Skills** list.
- Mirror the exact phrases used in real job posts you want.

> **Tip:** Keywords get you found; your niche statement and proof get you hired. Use both together.
