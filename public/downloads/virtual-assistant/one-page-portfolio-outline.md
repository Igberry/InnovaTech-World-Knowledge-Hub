# One-Page Portfolio Outline

> A simple one-page portfolio (a Notion page, a Canva one-pager, or a short PDF) is enough to start.
> Keep it clean and scannable. Use fictional or anonymised samples only.

## The one page, top to bottom

1. **Header:** your name, "Virtual Assistant", and your niche statement (one line)
2. **What I do:** 3 to 6 clear services (from your niche)
3. **Sample work:** 2 to 4 case studies (Problem → Action → Result) with a visual each
4. **Tools I use:** the tools you are comfortable with (Google Workspace, Slack, Trello, Canva, etc.)
5. **About me:** 3 to 4 honest lines, who you are, why you do this well, what it's like to work with you
6. **Contact / call to action:** exactly how to reach you (email, WhatsApp, booking link)

## Free ways to build it

- **Notion:** a single public page, easy to update
- **Canva:** a one-page design exported as PDF or a shareable link
- **A simple document** shared as a view-only link

See the [Remote Ready](/courses/remote-ready/) course for hosting a simple page for free.

## Checklist

- [ ] One page, clean and easy to scan
- [ ] Niche statement at the top
- [ ] 2 to 4 case studies with visuals
- [ ] Tools and a short about
- [ ] A clear, single call to action
- [ ] No real client private data anywhere

> **Tip:** Link your portfolio in your Upwork, LinkedIn, and marketplace profiles, and in every proposal.
> It is your single best piece of proof.
