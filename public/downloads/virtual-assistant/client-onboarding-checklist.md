# Client Onboarding Checklist

> Use this the moment a client says yes. A calm, organised start is the best first impression you can make.
> Keep each client's details private. Replace the [brackets].

## 1. Agree the basics (in writing, before any work)

- [ ] **Scope:** exactly what you will and will not do
- [ ] **Hours or package** and **rate** agreed in writing
- [ ] **Payment method and schedule** agreed (see Week 2 payments note)
- [ ] **Working hours, time zone, response time** confirmed
- [ ] **How you communicate** (tool) and **how often you report**
- [ ] **Who you report to** and who can give you tasks

## 2. Get access safely

- [ ] Access granted through each tool's own **share/invite** feature, not by emailing passwords
- [ ] You use a **password manager**; **2FA** on where possible
- [ ] You have **only the access you need**, nothing more

## 3. Set up the client's workspace

- [ ] A client folder (`01-Clients/[Client]/`) with sub-folders
- [ ] A task board/list for this client
- [ ] A time-tracking project for this client
- [ ] Saved SOPs/templates you'll reuse

## 4. First week

- [ ] Day 1: short kickoff, confirm priorities and the first three tasks
- [ ] Days 2 to 4: do the first tasks; ask questions early
- [ ] Day 5: send your first weekly report; ask for feedback

## 5. Kickoff questions to ask

1. What does a great first month look like to you?
2. What are the three things you most want off your plate?
3. How do you like to communicate, and how often?
4. What should I never do without checking first?
5. Who else will I work with, and how do I reach them?

> **Warning:** Never start real work before scope and payment are agreed in writing. And if a "client"
> wants you to receive or forward money, stop, see the scam checklist.
