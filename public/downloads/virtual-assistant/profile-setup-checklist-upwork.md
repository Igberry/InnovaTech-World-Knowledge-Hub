# Upwork Profile Setup Checklist

> Upwork is a large global freelance marketplace. As of November 2026 it is open to freelancers in Nigeria
> (and most countries). Rules, fees, and verification change, so check upwork.com for current details.
> This week you only **set up** the profile, you do not apply yet.

## Before you start

- [ ] Use your professional email and a clear, friendly headshot
- [ ] Have your niche statement (Week 1) ready, it becomes your headline and overview
- [ ] Have a valid ID ready, Upwork verifies identity (often with a live selfie matching your ID)

## Build the profile

- [ ] **Title/headline:** your niche in a few words (e.g., "Social Media VA for Coaches")
- [ ] **Overview:** expand your niche statement, who you help, what you do, your proof. Lead with the client's benefit.
- [ ] **Skills:** add the specific skills for your niche (use real keywords clients search)
- [ ] **Portfolio:** add samples as you build them in Week 4 (leave space for now)
- [ ] **Rate:** set a starting rate you researched (you refine this in Week 4)
- [ ] **Profile photo** and a short, professional bio tone

## Verify and finish

- [ ] Complete identity verification
- [ ] Reach 100% profile completeness if the platform shows a meter
- [ ] Turn on two-factor authentication

## Notes

- New accounts get a small number of free "Connects" (used to apply); more can be bought. You will use
  these from Week 3, not now.
- Verify current fees and Connect costs on Upwork before you rely on them.

> **Tip:** A specific, niche-led profile beats a generic "I do everything" one. Your Week 1 niche statement
> is your biggest advantage here.
