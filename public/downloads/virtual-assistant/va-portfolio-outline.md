# VA Portfolio Outline - Template

> A VA portfolio proves you can be trusted with real work, without sharing any client's private information.
> Use fictional or anonymised samples only. Replace every section below.

## 1. Header

- Your name and "Virtual Assistant"
- One line that says who you help and how (your positioning)
- A professional photo (optional) and how to contact you

## 2. Services

A short list of what you do (pull from your service menu). Three to six items.

## 3. Sample work (anonymised or fictional)

Show the work, not the client. For each sample:

- **What the task was** (e.g., "Inbox clean-up and SOP")
- **What I did** (two or three bullets)
- **The result** (e.g., "From 1,200 unread to zero, with a routine to keep it there")
- A screenshot or sample file **with all names and private details removed**

> **Privacy:** Never show a real client's emails, contacts, numbers, or internal documents. Recreate a sample with fictional data, or blur and remove every identifying detail. When in doubt, ask the client's permission in writing.

Suggested samples to build (even without a client yet):
- A tidy sample inbox with labels (using a practice account)
- A sample calendar for a fictional busy founder
- A filled-in SOP (use the Inbox and Calendar SOP template)
- A sample weekly client report (use the template, fictional data)

## 4. Tools I use

List the tools you are comfortable with (task boards, docs, scheduling, communication).

## 5. About me

Three or four lines: your background, why you do this well, and what it is like to work with you.

## 6. A clear call to action

Tell the reader exactly what to do next: "Email me at ___" or "Book a 20-minute call: ___".
