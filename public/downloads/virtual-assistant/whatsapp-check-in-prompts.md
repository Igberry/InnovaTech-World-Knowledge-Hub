# Daily WhatsApp Check-In Prompts

> From Week 3, you send your mentor a short daily "applications sent" message. From Week 5, applications go
> daily. The check-in keeps you accountable and lets your mentor help fast. Keep it to a few lines.

## The simple daily format

> **Date:** [today]
> **Applications sent:** [number] ([platforms])
> **Outreach sent:** [number]
> **Replies/interviews:** [any?]
> **Stuck on:** [one thing, or "nothing"]

## Example

> Mon 2 Feb
> Applications: 2 (Upwork, Fiverr)
> Outreach: 1 (LinkedIn)
> Replies: 1 asked for my rate
> Stuck on: how to answer the rate question

## Honest days are fine

- A "0 today because [reason]" message is still a check-in, send it. Hiding a slow day helps no one.
- Consistency over perfection: a steady 1 a day beats 10 in one burst then nothing.

## Prompts if you're stuck

- No time today? Send 1 application in 15 minutes using a saved proposal skeleton.
- No good jobs today? Send 1 cold outreach instead.
- Nervous about a reply? Paste it to your mentor and ask for a hand.

> **Tip:** Put a daily reminder on your phone for the check-in. The habit, not any single application, is
> what gets you hired.
