# VA Interview Question Bank

> Common questions VA clients ask, with how to approach each. Practise out loud before your mock interview.
> Answer using your niche statement and proof from your portfolio, not vague claims.

## About you and your fit

- **"Tell me about yourself."** 30 seconds: your niche, who you help, your proof. Not your life story.
- **"Why should I hire you?"** Mirror their need, then your matching proof. Use your niche statement.
- **"What's your experience?"** Frame transferable skills honestly as VA proof; show samples.
- **"What are your strengths and weaknesses?"** A real strength with proof; a genuine weakness plus how
  you manage it.

## About the work

- **"How do you handle multiple tasks/deadlines?"** Describe your system (task board, priorities, check-ins).
- **"What tools do you know?"** Name the ones you've used (Weeks 2 to 3); say you pick up new ones fast.
- **"How do you communicate with clients?"** Proactive updates, agreed response times, summaries in writing.
- **"What would you do if you didn't know how to do a task?"** Research first, then ask a clear question,
  never guess silently on something important.

## Logistics

- **"What are your hours / availability?"** Be clear and realistic about overlap with their time zone.
- **"What are your rates?"** State your researched rate calmly; don't apologise for it (see negotiation
  scripts).
- **"Can you do a trial?"** Be open to a **short, paid** trial; see the trial-task policy.

## Your questions for them (always ask some)

- What does a great first month look like to you?
- What are the top 3 tasks you want off your plate?
- How do you prefer to communicate, and how often?
- What's the scope, and how is success measured?

> **Tip:** Answer with specifics and proof, then stop. Rambling weakens a good answer. Practise until your
> core answers feel natural.
