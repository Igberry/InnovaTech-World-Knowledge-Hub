# VA Platforms and Agencies (Verified Notes, November 2026)

> Where VA work actually is, with honest flags on which accept Nigerian applicants. Platform rules change,
> so re-check each site before relying on it. Only well-known sites are linked.

## Marketplaces open to Nigerian freelancers (start here)

| Platform | Notes (checked Nov 2026) |
|---|---|
| [Upwork](https://www.upwork.com) | Global; open to Nigeria. Needs ID verification. Uses "Connects" to apply. |
| [Fiverr](https://www.fiverr.com) | Open to Nigeria; you create "gigs" (service packages). |
| [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com) | Free; some clients post VA roles and search for VAs directly. |

## VA-specific agencies (flagged)

| Agency | Flag (checked Nov 2026) |
|---|---|
| Belay | **US-based assistants only**, not open to Nigeria-based applicants. |
| Time Etc | **US (and historically UK) applicants**, not open to Nigeria. |
| Fancy Hands | **"All Fancy Hands assistants are US-based"**, not open to Nigeria. |

These are listed so you know them, but **do not spend time applying if you are in Nigeria**; verify
directly only if their policy appears to have changed.

## OnlineJobs.ph (important flag)

- [OnlineJobs.ph](https://www.onlinejobs.ph) is a major VA marketplace, but its jobseeker side **admits
  only Philippine workers** ("we only allow Philippine workers on the site," per its own blog). **Nigerian
  learners generally cannot register as workers.**
- You can still **read its job posts** to learn what clients want. Use it for research, not registration.

## Other places VA jobs appear

- **Facebook groups** for VAs and remote work (search for active, reputable groups; watch for scams).
- **LinkedIn job posts** and "Open to work."
- **Remote job boards** (see the Remote Ready course for vetted boards).

## Match platform to niche

- New + general: Upwork and Fiverr for first clients.
- Relationship/referral driven: LinkedIn and communities.
- Always point every profile and proposal at your **portfolio**.

> **Warning:** New VAs are heavily targeted by scams on every platform and in groups. Never pay to get a
> job, never handle a "client's" money, and verify who you deal with (see Week 6).
