# Niche Statement Template

> Your niche statement is one short paragraph that says: the type of VA work you do, who you do it for, and
> why you are credible. You will use it as your profile headline, your proposal opener, and your answer to
> "what do you do?" Draft it in Session B; finalise it for your Week 1 assignment.

## The shape

> "I help **[who]** with **[what VA work]**, so they can **[benefit]**. I bring **[your proof /
> transferable skill]**."

## Worked examples (write your own, do not copy)

- "I help busy coaches with inbox and calendar management, so they can focus on their clients. I bring two
  years of organising schedules and records for a small business."
- "I help small online stores with order processing and customer replies, so nothing slips and buyers
  stay happy. I bring experience running a busy group chat and keeping careful records."
- "I help real estate agents with listings admin and follow-ups, so no lead goes cold. I bring strong
  organisation and clear, friendly communication."

## Checklist for a strong statement

- [ ] Names a **specific** kind of client (not "anyone")
- [ ] Names **specific** VA work (not "all admin")
- [ ] States a clear **benefit** to the client
- [ ] Points to **proof** you can actually show or explain
- [ ] Reads as **one clear sentence or two**, out loud

> **Tip:** Your first statement is a starting point, not a life sentence. You will refine it in Week 4 when
> you optimise your LinkedIn profile.
