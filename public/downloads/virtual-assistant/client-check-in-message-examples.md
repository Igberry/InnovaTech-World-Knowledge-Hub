# Client Check-In Message Examples

> Proactive, predictable updates build trust and keep clients. Keep them short. Replace the [brackets].
> Send the same kinds of update at the same times each week.

## Start-of-week plan (Monday)

> Morning [name]! Here's my plan for this week: [top 3 priorities]. I'll send a mid-week update on
> Wednesday and a summary Friday. Anything you'd like me to add or reprioritise?

## Mid-week update (Wednesday)

> Quick update: [done so far]. On track for [the rest]. One thing I need from you: [specific ask, by when].

## End-of-week summary (Friday)

> This week: [results, with a number if you can]. In progress: [item]. Waiting on you: [specific]. Have a
> great weekend!

## "I noticed a problem" (as soon as it happens)

> Heads-up: I spotted [issue]. Here's what I've done so far: [action], and here's what I suggest next:
> [option]. Want me to go ahead, or handle it differently?

## Asking for feedback (end of first month)

> It's been a month, thank you! Is there anything you'd like me to do differently or more of? I want to
> make sure this is working well for you.

## Asking for a testimonial (after good work)

> I'm so glad that worked out. If you're happy, would you mind a couple of lines I could use as a
> testimonial? Happy to draft something you can edit, whatever's easiest.

> **Tip:** Lead with results, not activity. "Inbox at zero" beats "spent time on emails." Predictable
> updates mean a client rarely needs to chase you, which is exactly why they keep you.
