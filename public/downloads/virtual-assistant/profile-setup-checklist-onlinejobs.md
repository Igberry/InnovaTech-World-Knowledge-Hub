# Third-Profile Checklist (OnlineJobs.ph note + Nigeria-accessible alternative)

> **Important flag (checked November 2026):** OnlineJobs.ph is a major VA marketplace, but its jobseeker
> (worker) side **admits only Philippine workers** ("we only allow Philippine workers on the site," per
> OnlineJobs.ph's own blog). That means **a Nigeria-based learner generally cannot register as a jobseeker
> there.** Verify directly if you wish (support@onlinejobs.ph), but do not count on it.
>
> So for your **third live profile**, use a marketplace that accepts Nigerian freelancers. A good, verified
> option is **Fiverr** (open to Nigeria as of Nov 2026). Your mentor may suggest another. The checklist
> below works for Fiverr (or any similar marketplace).

## Know OnlineJobs.ph (awareness only)

- It is widely used by international clients hiring Philippine VAs.
- You may still **read** its job posts to learn what clients ask for, even if you cannot register as a
  worker. It is useful market research.

## Set up your third profile (Fiverr or similar)

- [ ] Sign up with your professional email; add a clear headshot
- [ ] **Create a "gig"** around your niche (what you offer, clearly, as a package)
- [ ] Write a benefit-led description using your niche statement
- [ ] Set a starting price you researched (refine in Week 4)
- [ ] Add samples as you build them (Week 4)
- [ ] Turn on two-factor authentication
- [ ] Confirm how you will get paid (see the payments note in Week 2's page)

## Notes

- Platform rules and fees change; check the platform's own site before relying on anything.
- The point of a third profile is simply more places for clients to find you, pick one you will actually
  maintain.

> **Tip:** Do not spread yourself across ten platforms. Three solid, complete profiles (Upwork, LinkedIn,
> and one marketplace) that you keep active beat ten half-finished ones.
