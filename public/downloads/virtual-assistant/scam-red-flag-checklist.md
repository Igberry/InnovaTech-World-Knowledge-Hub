# Scam Client Red-Flag Checklist (Printable)

> New VAs are heavily targeted by scams. Keep this handy and check any new "client" against it. If several
> boxes tick, stop and verify, or walk away. Losing a fake client costs nothing.

## Money red flags (the most dangerous)

- [ ] Asks you to **pay upfront** for "training materials", "software", or a "starter kit"
- [ ] **Overpays** then asks you to **refund the difference** (their payment later bounces, classic fraud)
- [ ] Wants you to **receive and forward money** on their behalf
- [ ] Pays, or asks you to pay, in **gift cards or crypto** for no clear reason
- [ ] Sends a **cheque to cash** and share part of

## Work red flags

- [ ] A long **"trial" that is really free project work**
- [ ] Vague about the actual tasks, scope, or who they are
- [ ] Pressures you to **start immediately** before anything is agreed in writing
- [ ] Refuses a call or any verification

## Identity red flags

- [ ] New account, no history, no verifiable business
- [ ] Contact details that don't match the "company"
- [ ] Communication only through odd channels, avoiding the platform

## What to do if unsure

1. **Slow down.** Urgency is the scammer's tool.
2. **Verify** the person/business independently (search them; ask for a call).
3. **Never** pay to get a job, refund an overpayment, or move money for a "client".
4. **Keep everything on the platform** where possible (it offers some protection).
5. **Report** suspicious clients to the platform, and ask your mentor if unsure.

> **Warning:** The one rule that defeats most scams: **a real client pays you for your work, you never pay
> them, refund them, or forward money for them.** If money flows the wrong way, it's a scam.
