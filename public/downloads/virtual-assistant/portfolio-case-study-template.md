# Portfolio Case-Study Template (Problem → Action → Result)

> A VA portfolio proves you can be trusted, even with no paid clients yet. The strongest format is a short
> case study for each sample: the Problem, your Action, and the Result. Use your Week 1 to 3 practice work.
> All samples must use fictional or fully anonymised data, never a real client's private information.

## The format (repeat per sample)

**Title:** [what the sample is, e.g., "Inbox clean-up and SOP"]

- **Problem:** [the situation, e.g., "A busy coach's inbox had 1,200 unread emails and missed bookings."]
- **Action:** [what you did, 2 to 3 bullets, e.g., "Set up labels and filters; built a daily triage
  routine; created an SOP."]
- **Result:** [the outcome, with a number if you can, e.g., "Inbox to zero, with a routine to keep it
  there; no missed bookings."]
- **Proof:** [a screenshot or sample file with all names/private details removed]

## Samples you can build from this course (no client needed)

- The **content calendar** (Week 3) → "Planned a week of social content for a fictional brand"
- The **customer-service templates** (Week 3) → "Created on-brand reply templates that cut response time"
- The **mock client workspace** (Week 2) → "Set up a complete, organised client workspace"
- The **expense tracker** (Week 3) → "Built a simple income-and-expense tracker"

## Checklist

- [ ] 2 to 4 case studies, each with Problem / Action / Result
- [ ] A visual or sample for each (fictional or anonymised)
- [ ] No real client names, emails, numbers, or private data anywhere
- [ ] A short "about me" and a clear way to contact you

> **Privacy:** When you later use real client work, recreate it with fictional data or get written
> permission and remove every identifying detail. Your reputation for discretion is part of your value.
