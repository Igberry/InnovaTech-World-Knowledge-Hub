# Cold Outreach Templates (LinkedIn, Email, Instagram)

> Cold outreach is messaging a potential client who didn't post a job. Done well, it's specific, short, and
> about them. Done badly, it's spam. Always personalise the first line. Replace every [bracket].

## The structure (all channels)

1. **Who you are** in one line
2. **What you noticed** about them (specific, shows you looked)
3. **The specific help** you offer (tied to what you noticed)
4. **Proof** (one line)
5. **One easy next step**

## LinkedIn (connection note or message)

> Hi [name], I help [who] with [what]. I noticed [specific thing about their business/page]. I think I could
> help by [specific help]. I've [one line of proof]. Open to a quick chat? Either way, love what you're
> doing with [specific].

## Email

> Subject: [specific idea] for [their business]
>
> Hi [name],
>
> I'm a [niche] VA who helps [who] with [what]. I noticed [specific observation about their work]. One quick
> idea: [specific help/idea].
>
> I've [proof, e.g., "built content calendars and simple graphics, samples here: link"]. If useful, I could
> [small next step, e.g., "send a short plan" or "do a quick sample"].
>
> Thanks for your time,
> [Your name]

## Instagram DM (short and warm)

> Hi [name]! Love your [specific post/thing]. I'm a [niche] VA, I help [who] with [what]. I had a quick idea
> for your [page/content]: [one specific thing]. Want me to share it?

## Rules

- **Personalise line 2 every time.** If it could be sent to anyone, don't send it.
- **Keep it short.** No one reads a long cold message.
- **Offer value, not just "hire me."** Lead with a useful idea.
- **One easy next step**, low pressure.
- **Don't spam.** A few well-researched messages beat fifty copy-pastes, and protect your accounts.

> **Warning:** Respect each platform's rules on messaging and don't mass-send identical DMs, it gets
> accounts restricted and annoys people. Quality and relevance win.
