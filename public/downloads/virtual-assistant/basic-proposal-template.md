# Basic Proposal / Application Template (Week 3)

> Use this for your first practice applications. It is a short, client-focused message. You will learn a
> stronger framework (the 5-Line Proposal) in Week 5; for now, practise the habit of applying with a clear,
> tailored message. Never send the same generic message to everyone.

## The shape

1. **A specific opener** that shows you read their post (mirror a detail or need)
2. **What you offer**, tied to their need (not a list of everything you do)
3. **Proof** (a transferable skill, a sample, or how you would approach it)
4. **One easy next step** (a question or a call)

## Template

> Hi [name],
>
> I saw you need [specific thing from their post]. [One sentence showing you understood their real
> problem.]
>
> I help [who] with [what], so [benefit]. For your [task], I would [one concrete thing you'd do].
>
> [Proof: a transferable skill, a relevant sample, or your approach in one line.]
>
> Would it help if I [small next step, e.g., sent a quick plan / hopped on a 15-minute call]?
>
> Thank you,
> [Your name], Virtual Assistant

## Do / don't

- **Do** tailor the opener to each post. The first line decides if they read on.
- **Do** keep it short, clients skim.
- **Don't** paste a generic "I am hardworking and dedicated" message.
- **Don't** list every service, match what they asked for.

> **Tip:** Log every application you send in your Application Tracker, and send your mentor a one-line
> WhatsApp check-in. Consistency is the goal this week, not perfection.
