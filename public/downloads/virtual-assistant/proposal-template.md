# Virtual Assistant Proposal - Template

> A proposal is a short, clear document that says: I understand your problem, here is how I will help, and here is what it costs.
> Keep it to one or two pages. Replace every sample line below with your own words.

---

**Prepared for:** [Client name]
**Prepared by:** [Your name], Virtual Assistant
**Date:** [Date]

## 1. What I understand you need

Two or three sentences showing you listened. Name their real problem in their words.

> Example: "You are spending two hours a day on email and scheduling, which pulls you away from client work. You want that time back and a calendar you can trust."

## 2. How I will help

List the specific services, not vague promises.

- Daily inbox triage and replies to routine messages
- Calendar management and scheduling
- A short written status report every Friday

## 3. What success looks like

Say how the client will know it is working.

- Inbox at zero unread by end of each day
- No double-booked meetings
- You spend no more than 15 minutes a day on admin

## 4. How we will work together

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Hours per month | [e.g., 20] |
| Communication | [e.g., chat tool, with a daily note] |
| My working hours | [your hours and time zone] |
| Response time | [e.g., within one business day] |
| Reporting | Weekly report every [day] |

## 5. Investment

State your price plainly. Use the figure you set in your pricing worksheet.

- [e.g., Monthly retainer: _______ for up to 20 hours]
- Payment: [e.g., at the start of each month]
- What happens if we go over the hours: [e.g., agreed in advance, billed at _______ per extra hour]

## 6. Next steps

1. You confirm you are happy with this proposal.
2. We agree a start date and sign a simple working agreement.
3. I send the onboarding checklist and we book a 20-minute kickoff.

---

*Thank you for considering working with me. Questions are welcome before you decide.*

> **Tip:** Send the proposal within a day of your discovery call, while the conversation is fresh. Follow up once, politely, if you do not hear back within a week.
