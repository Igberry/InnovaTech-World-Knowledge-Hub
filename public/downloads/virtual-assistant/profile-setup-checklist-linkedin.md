# LinkedIn Profile Setup Checklist

> LinkedIn is where many clients check you out and where some post VA work directly. It is free and open in
> Nigeria. This week you set up a solid base; you optimise it fully in Week 4.

## The essentials

- [ ] **Professional photo** (clear, friendly, well lit) and a simple background banner
- [ ] **Headline:** your niche, not just "Virtual Assistant" (e.g., "Virtual Assistant for E-commerce Stores | Orders, Listings, Support")
- [ ] **About section:** expand your niche statement, who you help, what you do, your proof, and a clear way to contact you
- [ ] **Experience:** add real and transferable experience (frame past work in VA terms)
- [ ] **Skills:** add niche-relevant skills (you add searchable keywords in Week 4)
- [ ] **Location and contact:** professional email included

## Settings

- [ ] Set your profile to public so clients can find it
- [ ] Turn on "Open to work" for the role types you want (optional)
- [ ] Turn on two-factor authentication

## Grow it

- [ ] Connect with people in your niche and in remote-work communities
- [ ] Follow pages relevant to your niche

> **Tip:** Recruiters and clients search LinkedIn by keywords. In Week 4 you will add the exact phrases
> they search for; for now, get a complete, professional base in place.
