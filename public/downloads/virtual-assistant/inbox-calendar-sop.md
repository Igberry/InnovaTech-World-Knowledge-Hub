## Part A - Inbox routine

### How often I check the inbox
- Morning pass: _______ (time)
- Midday pass: _______ (time)
- End-of-day pass: _______ (time)

### The four actions for every email (the 4 Ds)

| Action | When I use it | What I do |
|---|---|---|
| **Do** | Takes under 2 minutes | Handle it now, then file |
| **Delegate / escalate** | Needs the client or someone else | Forward with a one-line summary and what I need |
| **Defer** | Needs more than 2 minutes but is mine | Flag it, add to the task list, schedule time |
| **Delete / archive** | No action needed | File it away so the inbox stays clean |

### Labels / folders I use
- [ ] Action needed
- [ ] Waiting on reply
- [ ] Receipts / finance
- [ ] Reference
- [ ] Done / archived

### Replies I can send without asking (agree these with the client)
- Example: "Thanks, received - we will reply by <date>."
- Example: Scheduling a call using the client's booking link.
- Yours: _______________________

### Things I must ALWAYS check with the client first
- Anything about money, contracts, or refunds
- Anything from the client's boss, biggest client, or family
- Anything I am unsure about

> **Rule of thumb:** When in doubt, ask. One quick question is cheaper than one wrong email sent on the client's behalf.

## Part B - Calendar routine

### Rules
- Default meeting length: _______ minutes
- Buffer between meetings: _______ minutes
- Protected focus time (no meetings): _______
- Days or hours that are always off-limits: _______

### Booking a meeting - my checklist
- [ ] Confirm the time zone of everyone invited
- [ ] Add a clear title: who + what (e.g., "InnovaTech x Client - intro call")
- [ ] Add the video link or location
- [ ] Add an agenda or purpose in the notes
- [ ] Add a reminder
- [ ] Check it does not clash or break a focus block

### Rescheduling - my checklist
- [ ] Offer two or three new times
- [ ] Update all attendees
- [ ] Note the reason briefly (for my own records)

---

## Part C - Handover notes

At the end of each day I send the client a short note:

- What I handled
- What is waiting on them (with a clear ask)
- Anything urgent for tomorrow

> **Tip:** Keep the daily note to five lines or fewer. Busy clients read short notes; long ones get ignored.
