# Prompt Pattern Library - Starter Templates

> A prompt is the instruction you give an AI tool. Good prompts get good results.
> These are reusable patterns. Replace the [square brackets] with your own details.
> Always read the AI's output critically and check facts (see the fact-check worksheet).

## The core shape of a strong prompt

Most good prompts have four parts. You do not always need all four, but more detail usually helps:

1. **Role / context:** who the AI should act as, and the situation
2. **Task:** exactly what you want done
3. **Details / input:** the material, constraints, length, and format
4. **Output format:** how you want the answer (list, table, email, steps)

**Example:**
> You are a helpful assistant for a small business in Yenagoa. Write a short, friendly WhatsApp
> message [task] telling customers our shop will be closed next Monday for a public holiday [details].
> Keep it under 40 words, warm but professional [constraints].

## Pattern 1: Draft then improve

> Write a first draft of [thing]. Keep it [length/tone].

Then follow up:
> Make it shorter and clearer. / Make the tone warmer. / Fix any grammar.

## Pattern 2: Explain simply

> Explain [topic] in plain language, as if to someone with no background in it.
> Use one everyday example. Keep it under [N] words.

## Pattern 3: Summarise

> Summarise the text below into [N] bullet points, keeping only the key facts.
> Do not add anything that is not in the text.
>
> Text: [paste text]

## Pattern 4: Rewrite / change tone

> Rewrite the message below to be [more formal / friendlier / shorter].
> Keep the meaning the same.
>
> Message: [paste message]

## Pattern 5: Brainstorm options

> Give me [N] different ideas for [goal]. For each, add one line on why it could work.

## Pattern 6: Compare

> Compare [A] and [B] in a table, with columns for [criteria]. Keep it factual.

## Pattern 7: Step-by-step plan

> Break [goal] into clear, numbered steps a beginner could follow.

## Pattern 8: Check my work

> Here is my [text/plan]. Point out weaknesses, unclear parts, and anything missing.
> Be honest and specific.
>
> [paste your work]

## Tips that improve any prompt

- **Give an example** of what good looks like ("in the style of this: ...").
- **Say what to avoid** ("do not use jargon", "no emoji").
- **Ask for the format** you want (table, list, 3 sentences).
- **Iterate.** The first answer is a draft. Ask for changes.
- **Give feedback** ("too long", "more formal") rather than starting over.

> **Warning:** AI tools can sound confident and still be wrong. Never trust facts, numbers, names,
> quotes, laws, or links from an AI without checking them yourself. Use the fact-check worksheet.

> **Privacy:** Never paste passwords, bank details, someone's private information, or confidential
> work into an AI tool. Assume anything you type could be stored. See the responsible-use checklist.
