# Responsible AI Use and Privacy Checklist

> AI tools are powerful, but using them well means using them honestly and safely.
> Use this checklist before and while you use any AI tool. Tick what applies.

## Before I paste anything in

- [ ] I am **not** pasting passwords, bank or payment details, or ID numbers
- [ ] I am **not** pasting someone else's private information without their consent
- [ ] I am **not** pasting confidential or client work that I am not allowed to share
- [ ] If the information is sensitive, I have removed or changed the identifying details

> **Privacy:** Assume anything you type into an AI tool could be stored or seen by others. When in
> doubt, leave it out, or replace real names and numbers with fake ones.

## Accuracy (do not trust blindly)

- [ ] I treat the AI's answer as a draft, not a fact
- [ ] I have checked any facts, numbers, names, quotes, laws, or links myself
- [ ] For anything important (health, money, legal, safety), I checked a trusted human source

> **Warning:** AI tools "hallucinate" - they can invent facts, sources, and quotes that look real.
> Confidence is not correctness.

## Honesty and fairness

- [ ] I am not passing off AI work as my own where that would be dishonest (school, exams, clients)
- [ ] If disclosure is expected, I have said where I used AI
- [ ] I have checked the output for bias or unfair assumptions and fixed them
- [ ] I am not using AI to deceive, impersonate, or harm anyone

## Copyright and rights

- [ ] For AI images or text I will publish or sell, I checked the tool's usage rights
- [ ] I am not copying someone's work or style in a way that breaks their rights
- [ ] I credit sources where I should

## Good habits

- [ ] I keep learning the underlying skill, so I can judge the AI, not just rely on it
- [ ] I keep a note of prompts that worked (see the prompt log)
- [ ] I stop and ask a person when the stakes are high
