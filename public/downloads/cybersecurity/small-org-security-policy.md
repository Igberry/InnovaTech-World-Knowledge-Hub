# Small Organisation Security Policy - Starter Template

> A short, plain security policy helps a small business or team protect itself and its customers.
> This is a starter you adapt, not legal advice. Keep it short enough that people will actually follow it.
> Replace every [bracket].

## 1. Purpose

This policy helps [organisation name] keep its data, accounts, and customers safe. Everyone who works here
follows it.

## 2. Accounts and passwords

- Everyone uses a **password manager** and a **unique, strong password** per account.
- **Two-step verification (2FA)** is on for all work accounts.
- Accounts are **personal**; we do not share logins. Shared tools use proper team access.
- When someone leaves, their access is **removed the same day**.

## 3. Access (least privilege)

- People get **only the access they need** for their role, nothing more.
- Access to sensitive data (customer details, finances) is limited and reviewed [how often].
- Admin rights are given sparingly and tracked.

## 4. Devices

- Work devices lock with a PIN/password and auto-update.
- Reputable security software is used where appropriate.
- Lost or stolen devices are reported immediately to [contact].

## 5. Data and privacy

- Customer and staff data is treated as confidential and shared only as needed.
- Sensitive data is not sent through insecure or personal channels.
- We keep only the data we need, and dispose of it safely.
- Backups follow our [backup plan].

## 6. Phishing and scams

- Staff are trained to spot phishing (see the phishing cheat-sheet).
- Suspicious messages are reported to [contact], not actioned.
- No one shares passwords or one-time codes, ever, with anyone.
- Payment or bank-detail changes are verified by a second trusted channel.

## 7. If something goes wrong

- Report any suspected incident to [contact] immediately. Reporting early is encouraged, never punished.
- We follow our incident-response plan.

## 8. Review

- This policy is reviewed [every 6 or 12 months] and after any incident.

Signed / agreed by the team on: _______________________

> **Note:** A policy only works if people understand and follow it. Keep it short, explain the why, and
> make reporting easy and blame-free, people hide mistakes when they fear punishment, which makes things
> worse.
