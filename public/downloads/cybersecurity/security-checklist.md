# Personal Security Hardening Checklist

> Work through this to make your own accounts and devices much harder to attack. Do a few items a day.
> Tick what you have done. Revisit every few months. This is about protecting yourself, never attacking
> anyone else.

## Accounts and passwords

- [ ] I use a password manager (one strong master password; it remembers the rest)
- [ ] Every important account has a different, strong password
- [ ] Two-step verification (2FA) is on for email, banking, and social accounts
- [ ] I use an authenticator app or passkeys where possible (stronger than SMS codes)
- [ ] My email account (the key to password resets) is especially well protected
- [ ] I have removed old accounts I no longer use

## Devices

- [ ] My phone and laptop lock with a PIN, password, or biometrics
- [ ] Automatic software and app updates are on
- [ ] I only install apps from official stores or trusted sources
- [ ] I have reputable security software where appropriate
- [ ] My device screen locks quickly when idle

## Data and backups

- [ ] Important files are backed up in at least two places (e.g., device + cloud)
- [ ] I have tested that I can actually restore a backup
- [ ] Sensitive files are protected (strong account passwords, device encryption if available)
- [ ] I know how to wipe a device before selling or giving it away

## Everyday habits

- [ ] I pause before clicking links or opening attachments I did not expect
- [ ] I check website addresses before entering passwords or payment details
- [ ] I do not reuse passwords across sites
- [ ] I am careful what I share publicly (travel, location, personal details)
- [ ] I do not enter sensitive details on public or shared computers

## If something goes wrong

- [ ] I know the first steps if an account is hacked (change password, turn on 2FA, check recovery options)
- [ ] I know who to contact (bank, platform support) and how to report it
- [ ] I have an incident-response plan (see the template)

> **Rule of thumb:** You do not need to be perfectly secure, you need to be a harder target than the next
> person, and to recover quickly if something slips through. Small habits add up to real protection.

> **Important:** Everything here is about protecting your own accounts and devices. Never use any technique
> to access accounts, devices, or data that are not yours.
