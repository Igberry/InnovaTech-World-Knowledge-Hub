# Backup Plan Template

> A backup is a spare copy of your important data, so a lost phone, a broken laptop, theft, or ransomware
> does not mean losing everything. Fill this in and test it. An untested backup is not a backup.

## The 3-2-1 idea (a simple, strong target)

- **3** copies of important data
- on **2** different types of storage (e.g., your device + cloud, or device + external drive)
- with **1** copy kept somewhere separate (off-site or cloud), in case of fire, theft, or flood

You do not need expensive tools. A free cloud account plus your device can meet most of this.

## Make it automatic where you can

- Phone photos and contacts: turn on backup/sync to a cloud account
- Documents: save or sync to cloud automatically
- A reminder for anything you must back up by hand

## Test it (the step most people skip)

- [ ] I have actually **restored** a file from each backup to confirm it works
- [ ] I know how to recover everything if my main device is lost today
- [ ] My backups are protected (strong account password, 2FA)

> **Warning:** Ransomware and theft can take your main device and anything connected to it. Keep at least
> one backup that is not always connected (a separate cloud account or an external drive you unplug).

> **Important:** Back up before you need it. The best time to set this up is now, not after something goes
> wrong.
