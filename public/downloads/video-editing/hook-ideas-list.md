# Short-form hook prompts

These are prompts, not claims. Adapt them to footage you own and information you can support.

- “Here is one small detail that changes the result…”
- “Watch this process from start to finish.”
- “Before the final version, we changed one thing…”
- “Three steps we use to prepare this item.”
- “A close look at the materials behind this project.”
- “What happens between the first cut and the finished edit?”
- “One way to make this instruction easier to follow.”
- “A quiet behind-the-scenes moment from a practice shoot.”
- “This is the difference between the rough cut and the final cut.”
- “A quick tour of the fictional Fresh Bites sample menu.”

Check that the hook matches the video and does not promise a result, invent a customer story, or reveal private information.
