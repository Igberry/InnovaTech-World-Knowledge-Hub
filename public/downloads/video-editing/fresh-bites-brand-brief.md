# Fictional Fresh Bites brand brief

**Practice business:** Fresh Bites is fictional. It is a small Yenagoa food business invented for this course. It has no real address, phone number, customers, prices or operating history.

## Audience and message

- **Audience:** people who want a convenient, clearly described meal.
- **Goal:** introduce one fictional menu item and invite the viewer to learn more.
- **Tone:** warm, clear, tidy and trustworthy; no exaggerated health, quality or delivery claims.
- **Call to action:** “See the sample menu” or “Ask about the menu” (do not add a real number or address).

## Visual direction

- Use simple, high-contrast text and clean backgrounds.
- Create a fictional wordmark in text or use the supplied simple SVG.
- Use your own footage of your own food, craft or product; do not imply it is Fresh Bites footage from a real business.
- Avoid copied brand marks, stock images without a suitable licence, and identifiable people without permission.
- Keep titles and captions clear on a small phone screen; avoid flashing effects.

## Fictional menu example

“Fresh Bites Sample Bowl” is a fictional name. Do not invent a real price, nutritional claim, customer quote, delivery area or order channel.
