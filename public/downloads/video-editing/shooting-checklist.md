# Phone shooting checklist

## Before filming

- [ ] Confirm the subject, purpose and permission.
- [ ] Check the location is safe and filming is allowed.
- [ ] Charge the phone; check free storage and battery.
- [ ] Clean the lens and record a short test.
- [ ] Choose one orientation for the planned sequence.
- [ ] Find soft light; avoid a bright window directly behind the subject.
- [ ] Reduce background noise; keep the phone close to the sound source.
- [ ] Frame the shot and check the background for addresses, screens and private details.
- [ ] Plan wide, medium, close-up and detail coverage.

## During each clip

- [ ] Hold the camera steadily and keep the horizon intentional.
- [ ] Start recording before the action and hold after it.
- [ ] Record 5–10 seconds or longer where the action needs it.
- [ ] Avoid unsafe movement, hot surfaces, roads, cables and crowded paths.
- [ ] Capture room tone where appropriate.
- [ ] Do not record identifiable people without permission.

## After filming

- [ ] Play back sample clips and check picture and sound.
- [ ] Copy originals to `01-Raw`; do not overwrite or delete them.
- [ ] Make and test a second copy.
- [ ] Record any missing shots before the location or light changes.
- [ ] Secure the phone and return the location to its original state.
