# Video Editing capstone rubric

Score each area from 0 to 4: 0 = missing or unsafe; 1 = needs substantial work; 2 = developing; 3 = meets the brief; 4 = strong and deliberate.

| Area | Weight | Evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Editing craft | 35% | Clear cuts, pacing, sound, captions, titles and exports |
| Story and brief fit | 20% | A coherent idea and appropriate content for the intended viewer |
| Workflow and documents | 20% | Organised folder, versions, brief, licence log and delivery note |
| Licensing and ethics | 10% | Permission, privacy and asset-use records are complete |
| Presentation | 15% | Explains decisions, limitations and next improvements clearly |

**Weighted score:** add `(area score ÷ 4) × weight` for each area. A project with unresolved permission or licensing concerns must be corrected before public sharing, regardless of score.
