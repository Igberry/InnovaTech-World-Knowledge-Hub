# Filming consent and location permission

Use this checklist before filming someone who can be identified or a location that is private or controlled. It is a practical reminder, not a legal contract or legal advice.

## Explain before asking

- Who is making the video and why?
- What will be filmed, and will the person's face, voice, name or work be identifiable?
- Is this only a private class exercise, a portfolio sample, a public post, or client work?
- Where might the video appear, and who may see it?
- How can the person ask a question or change their mind before publication?
- Will the video be edited, captioned, shortened or combined with other material?

## Permission check

- [ ] The person understood the intended use and freely agreed.
- [ ] The location owner or organiser approved filming where needed.
- [ ] Any venue or event rules have been checked.
- [ ] A guardian has given permission before filming a child; the child is not pressured.
- [ ] No private screen, address, document, badge, payment detail or private conversation is captured.
- [ ] Permission and any limits are noted in the project folder.
- [ ] A person who declines is not filmed, recorded in the background or used in B-roll.

## If permission is unclear

Do not record or publish the material. Change the frame, use your own object or product, film an empty space, or make a text storyboard instead. For a real client or public release, use an appropriate written release reviewed by the responsible organisation; this one-pager is not a release form.
