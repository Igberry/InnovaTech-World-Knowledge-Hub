# Caption style guide

- Use sentence case and ordinary punctuation.
- Keep each caption short enough to read while watching; split long sentences at natural pauses.
- Proofread names, local words, numbers and punctuation manually, even if captions were generated automatically.
- Use a solid or semi-opaque background when the footage makes text hard to read.
- Keep strong contrast and a readable size; check on a small phone screen.
- Place captions away from the screen edges and areas covered by platform controls. Check the current app preview rather than relying on a memorised safe-zone diagram.
- Identify meaningful speakers when it helps understanding; do not label every sound.
- Include important sound cues when they change the meaning, such as “[door closes]”.
- Avoid flashing, rapid strobing or colour-only distinctions.
- Export a captioned review copy and, where supported, a separate caption file.
