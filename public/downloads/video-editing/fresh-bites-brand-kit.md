# Fictional Fresh Bites brand kit

Fresh Bites is a fictional classroom case. Do not present this as a real business identity.

## Simple rules

- **Voice:** friendly, plain and specific.
- **Audience:** a viewer considering a sample menu.
- **Palette:** use dark navy text on a light neutral background; use one bright accent sparingly. Check contrast before exporting.
- **Typography:** choose one readable sans-serif family available on the device; do not embed a font unless its licence permits it.
- **Logo:** use the supplied `fresh-bites-logo.svg` or plain text. Do not add a real address, logo, phone number or invented testimonial.
- **Photography:** learner-owned or explicitly permitted footage only.
- **Claims:** do not make health, quality, customer-count, delivery or price claims without evidence.

## End-card sample

**FRESH BITES**
