# Fictional client brief: community choir promo

This is a fictional classroom exercise. It does not describe a real church, choir, venue, event or contact.

## Request

Create a short promotional video for a fictional community choir showcase. The purpose is to explain that a performance is planned and invite viewers to learn more from the organiser. Do not invent an actual date, venue, ticket price, donation link or contact method.

## Constraints

- Use your own footage of a consenting adult performer, or use hands, instruments and empty-room details.
- Do not record children, worshippers or private conversations.
- Use only music written and performed by the learner or explicitly licensed for this use. The choir's performance itself may be copyrighted; obtain permission.
- Create one review version and apply the two supplied revision notes.
- Include readable captions for any spoken words and a clear fictional end-card.
- Keep a licence log and a delivery note.

## Required deliverables

1. One project plan and shot list.
2. Version 1, version 2 and version 3 exports or review files.
3. A change log explaining how each revision was handled.
4. A one-page project report.
