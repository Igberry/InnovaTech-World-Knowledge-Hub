# Interview question bank

Use only with a consenting adult who understands the project's purpose. Avoid questions that pressure someone to reveal private, financial, health or identifying details.

## Process and craft

- What are you making or demonstrating?
- What is the first step?
- Which part requires the most care?
- How do you know this step is complete?
- What is one detail a viewer might miss?

## Event or project story

- What is the purpose of this event or project?
- What moment best represents it?
- What should viewers understand afterwards?
- Is there a detail you would like us to leave out?
- How would you describe the result in your own words?

Ask one question at a time, listen without interrupting, and allow the interviewee to decline. Confirm pronunciation of names and ask permission before recording. Do not invent or edit an answer to imply something the person did not say.
