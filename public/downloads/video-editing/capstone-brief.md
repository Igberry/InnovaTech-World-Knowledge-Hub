# Video Editing capstone brief

Create a portfolio package for a real client only with written permission, or use a fictional client. Do not publish a real person's image, voice, private location or contact information without consent.

## Required package

1. A 60–90 second showreel with three to five permitted pieces.
2. One two-to-three-minute long-form story or event highlight.
3. Three short-form videos with clear captions and calls to action.
4. A project folder with brief, quote worksheet, licence log and delivery note.
5. A ten-minute presentation explaining your planning, edits, permissions, feedback and next improvements.

## Suggested workflow

Write the brief and confirm permissions. Plan shots and story. Capture or select permitted footage. Keep original files and backups. Edit a rough cut, request focused feedback, make agreed changes, caption and review, export and test. Prepare the licence and delivery documents before presenting.

## Boundaries

- No client pricing or sensitive documents are included in this course.
- Do not use commercial music without a licence for the intended use.
- Do not promise audience growth, sales, jobs or income.
- Respect privacy and client retention/deletion instructions.
