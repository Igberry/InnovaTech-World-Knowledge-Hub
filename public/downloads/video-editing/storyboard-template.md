## Coverage check

- [ ] Opening establishes the subject.
- [ ] Wide, medium and close-up coverage are planned where useful.
- [ ] B-roll can cover edits or explain the process.
- [ ] The planned camera position is safe and permitted.
- [ ] No unnecessary private or identifying detail is included.
