## Quote checklist

- [ ] Deliverables, duration and destinations are described.
- [ ] Revision count and what counts as a new request are clear.
- [ ] Client supplies or approves facts, logos and permissions.
- [ ] Deposit, payment timing, cancellation and ownership terms are written.
- [ ] Usage rights, raw files, archive and deletion responsibilities are stated.
- [ ] No promise of views, sales, audience growth or results is made.

Ask a qualified local adviser to review legal or tax questions.
