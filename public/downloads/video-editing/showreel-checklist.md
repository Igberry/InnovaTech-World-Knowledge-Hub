# Showreel checklist

- [ ] Choose three to five strong pieces that you have permission to show.
- [ ] Put the clearest work first; do not use a long logo animation.
- [ ] Keep the whole reel between 60 and 90 seconds for this course brief.
- [ ] Use only music licensed for public portfolio use; record it in the licence log.
- [ ] Add captions or a transcript for spoken content.
- [ ] Keep titles readable and use a simple, accurate role description.
- [ ] Remove client names, contact details or unreleased work unless approved.
- [ ] Add a contact end-card only with details you choose to publish.
- [ ] Watch the final export on a phone and another screen if available.
- [ ] Check hosting visibility and access before sharing a link.
- [ ] Keep a local master and a separate backup.
