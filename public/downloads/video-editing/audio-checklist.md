# Audio recording and mix checklist

## Voice recording

- [ ] Choose a quiet room and pause avoidable noise.
- [ ] Place the microphone close enough for clear speech without entering the frame.
- [ ] Record a short test; listen with headphones before continuing.
- [ ] Speak naturally, keep a steady distance and leave short pauses between sections.
- [ ] Record a few seconds of room tone.
- [ ] Re-record unclear names or phrases rather than hiding them with effects.

## Editing and mix

- [ ] Dialogue is intelligible at a comfortable listening level.
- [ ] No peaks sound clipped, harsh or distorted.
- [ ] Music and effects do not compete with speech.
- [ ] Ducking or manual volume automation is checked by listening, not just watching meters.
- [ ] Music and sound effects have a logged licence that permits the intended use.
- [ ] The final file is checked on headphones and a phone speaker where available.
