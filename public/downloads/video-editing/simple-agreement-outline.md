# Simple video project agreement outline

This outline is for learning only and is not a contract or legal advice. Have a qualified local professional review an agreement before using it with a real client.

1. Parties and approved contact people.
2. Project purpose, audience and approved facts.
3. Deliverables, formats, destinations and due dates.
4. Client-provided footage, claims, logos and proof of rights.
5. Filming permissions, location access and participant releases.
6. Price, deposit, payment milestones, taxes and expenses.
7. Number of revision rounds, review deadlines and out-of-scope changes.
8. Music, graphics, font and footage licences.
9. Ownership and permitted usage of final files and project files.
10. Delivery method, raw-file handover and archive/deletion schedule.
11. Cancellation, delay, force majeure and dispute process.
12. Signatures and date.

Do not use this outline to collect sensitive identity details or as a substitute for legal review.
