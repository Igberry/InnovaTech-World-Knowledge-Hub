# Ten-clip shot list

Use this list for the fictional Fresh Bites practice case or a safe, consented subject you own or have permission to film. Record each shot for at least 5–10 seconds, hold still before and after the action, and capture both horizontal and vertical versions of three selected clips. Do not film a person without clear permission.
