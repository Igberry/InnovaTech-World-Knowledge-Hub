# Edit review checklist

Review a copy of the edit, not the only project file. Watch once without pausing, then review picture, sound, story and delivery separately.

## Story and timing

- [ ] The opening makes the subject clear.
- [ ] Every shot adds information or supports the mood.
- [ ] Cuts begin and end at useful moments; no accidental gaps remain.
- [ ] The pacing suits the action rather than forcing every shot to be fast.
- [ ] The ending feels deliberate.

## Picture and text

- [ ] Exposure, focus and framing are usable.
- [ ] Reframing does not cut off important content.
- [ ] Titles are readable, high contrast and inside safe margins.
- [ ] Captions are proofread and timed to the spoken words.
- [ ] No flashing effect or colour-only instruction is used.

## Sound and permissions

- [ ] Speech is understandable and no peak sounds distorted.
- [ ] Music does not mask speech and is licensed for the intended use.
- [ ] Licence log has a source, terms, date and attribution requirements.
- [ ] Everyone identifiable gave permission for this use.
- [ ] No private details appear in picture, captions or sound.

## Export and project

- [ ] The export plays from beginning to end on a second player if possible.
- [ ] Aspect ratio and dimensions match the intended destination.
- [ ] The file opens and the size is appropriate for the delivery method.
- [ ] Project file, source footage and export are named and stored correctly.
- [ ] A second copy exists and has been checked.
