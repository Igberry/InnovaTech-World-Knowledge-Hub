# Video project discovery questions

Ask only for information needed to plan the work. Do not collect account passwords or unnecessary personal data.

1. What should the video help the audience understand or do?
2. Who is the intended viewer?
3. Where will the video be used? Who controls those accounts?
4. What facts, dates, claims or calls to action are approved?
5. What footage and brand assets are available, and who owns or licensed them?
6. Who can appear on camera, and how will permission be recorded?
7. Are there people, locations or subjects that must not be filmed?
8. What captions, language, transcript or accessibility needs should be planned?
9. Which aspect ratios and delivery formats are required? Who will confirm current platform specifications?
10. Who reviews the first cut, and how many revision rounds are included?
11. What is the deadline and what dependencies could delay it?
12. How should final files be delivered, retained or deleted?

Summarise the answers in a written brief and get approval before filming.
