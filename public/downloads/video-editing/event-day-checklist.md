# Event filming checklist

Use only with organiser approval and clear permission from identifiable participants. Follow venue rules and never obstruct an activity.

## Before leaving

- [ ] Confirm shot list, filming areas and no-film areas with the organiser.
- [ ] Check phone battery, storage, microphone and safe transport.
- [ ] Pack a charger or power bank and a safe cable plan.
- [ ] Confirm who will handle footage and how it will be backed up.
- [ ] Review consent needs, especially for children and private events.

## On site

- [ ] Check the camera position is safe and does not block exits or walkways.
- [ ] Confirm permission before close-ups or interviews.
- [ ] Capture establishing, medium and detail shots without filming private screens.
- [ ] Record a short audio test and room tone where appropriate.
- [ ] Keep equipment attended and secure.
- [ ] Do not fly a drone or use equipment in a way the venue prohibits.

## After the event

- [ ] Thank participants and respect any withdrawal request before publication.
- [ ] Copy files to the agreed secure location.
- [ ] Verify a second copy before clearing the phone.
- [ ] Record asset permissions and licence terms.
- [ ] Follow the agreed retention and deletion schedule.
