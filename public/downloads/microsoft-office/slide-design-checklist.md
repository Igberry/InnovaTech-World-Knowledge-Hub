# PowerPoint design and delivery checklist

## Before building

- [ ] One clear audience and purpose
- [ ] A simple story: problem, solution, proof or example, next step
- [ ] A short outline before choosing decoration
- [ ] Any data, amount, quotation or social-proof sentence is verified or visibly labelled fictional

## Slide design

- [ ] One main idea per slide, supported by a specific headline
- [ ] Consistent Slide Master, layouts, colours and type hierarchy
- [ ] Readable text with adequate contrast and sufficient size for the room
- [ ] Images and icons are licensed or explicitly permitted; informative images have alt text
- [ ] Tables and charts have labels, units and a source or fictional-data note
- [ ] Enough white space; no unnecessary 3-D effects or decorative clutter
- [ ] Animations and transitions are restrained and do not delay understanding
- [ ] No important meaning depends on colour alone

## Rehearsal and sharing

- [ ] Speaker notes contain prompts, not a paragraph to read word-for-word
- [ ] Slide show and Presenter View work on the device being used
- [ ] The talk fits its time limit after a rehearsal
- [ ] Online screen sharing shows the slide show, not private notes or unrelated windows
- [ ] Exported PDF opens and contains no clipped text
- [ ] Video export, if used, includes correct timing and accessible captions where available
- [ ] Filename follows the project naming convention and sharing permissions are appropriate
