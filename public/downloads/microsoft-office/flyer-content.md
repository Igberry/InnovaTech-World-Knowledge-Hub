## Design reminders

- Use one clear heading and a short supporting line.
- Make the intended next step obvious without pretending that this practice offer is available.
- Keep all text readable and leave adequate space around the edge.
- Add a real image only if you have permission to use it; otherwise use a labelled placeholder.
- Check spelling, dates, currency and contact details before any genuine publication.
