# Mail merge letter starter

Use **customers-list-10.csv** only as fictional practice data. The `.invalid` email addresses are reserved examples; do not send messages to them. In Word, choose the Mailings tab and connect the CSV as a recipient list. Insert merge fields using the command in the app rather than typing the braces as if they were actual fields.

```text
FRESH BITES (FICTIONAL)
Yenagoa, Bayelsa State

12 November 2026

Dear «FirstName»,

We are practising a clear, helpful message for a fictional catering service. The «PreferredPackage» is a sample menu idea only and is not an available offer or confirmed order.

For a real enquiry, we would confirm the event date, guest count, menu, dietary requirements, delivery details and current written quotation before agreeing to any service. No personal or payment information is needed for this training exercise.

Thank you for reviewing this sample.

Kind regards,

Amaka Okorie
Practice Coordinator
Fresh Bites (fictional)
```

Before completing a real mail merge, preview every recipient record, inspect punctuation and blank fields, and obtain approval for both the recipient list and message. Do not send a practice merge.
