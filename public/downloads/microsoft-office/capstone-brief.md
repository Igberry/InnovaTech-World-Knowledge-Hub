# Office Admin Pack capstone brief

## Practice case

This is a fictional learning exercise for Yenagoa Fresh Bites. All people, clients, customer records and financial amounts are invented. The pack is not a real business proposal, order, testimonial, budget or service offer.

## Required portfolio pieces

1. **Word:** a formal supplier letter and a mail merge review copy for the ten fictional records. Include a clean proposal or document copy. Do not send the merged letters.
2. **Excel:** a monthly budget workbook and sales workbook using the provided practice data; include a chart and a simple PivotTable. Keep the starter data unchanged on its own sheet.
3. **PowerPoint:** a consistent ten-slide pitch deck with speaker notes and a rehearsed one-minute talk. Label all sample figures and role-play feedback as fictional.
4. **Outlook and OneNote:** prepare a team meeting in Outlook with a clear subject, time zone and agenda; record the minutes and actions in OneNote. Use a draft or an authorised test participant. Do not invite a real person without their consent.
5. **Office standards guide:** one page describing naming, folders, versioning, templates and safe sharing for the fictional team.

## File naming and evidence

Save each editable artifact with a date-first name and version number. Keep a private local working copy and create PDFs or screenshots only when they help a reviewer. Crop screenshots to the relevant work and remove account addresses, private notifications and unrelated files. Do not submit a password, verification code, personal customer list or other sensitive data.

## Review checklist

- [ ] Each item opens in its intended app or a clearly identified alternative.
- [ ] The chosen tool fits the task and file versions are labelled consistently.
- [ ] Spreadsheet totals and chart labels are checked against the source data.
- [ ] The Word merge is previewed but not sent.
- [ ] The deck's sample amounts and role-play sentence are visibly fictional.
- [ ] The meeting uses a safe practice recipient or remains an unsent draft.
- [ ] The OneNote page records decisions and action owners without private information.
- [ ] The standards guide is readable, one page and actionable.
