# Fresh Bites supplier letter content

**Practice-only scenario.** Fresh Bites and Creekside Produce Co. are fictional. The example is not an order, quotation request or real supplier recommendation.

```text
FRESH BITES
Yenagoa, Bayelsa State

12 November 2026

The Sales Coordinator
Creekside Produce Co. (fictional)

Subject: Request for product and delivery information

Dear Sales Coordinator,

Fresh Bites is preparing a fictional catering proposal for a training exercise. We would appreciate a current product list showing the item name, unit or pack size, any availability notes, and the delivery lead time you would normally confirm before an order.

For the practice table, learners may use these sample items and amounts: rice, ₦42,000 per bag; tomatoes, ₦18,500 per crate; chicken, ₦7,400 per kilogram; and vegetable oil, ₦6,900 per bottle. These figures are invented solely for the exercise and are not current market prices or a supplier quotation.

Please identify any assumptions or substitutions rather than treating an unavailable item as confirmed. No payment or purchase is being requested. In a real business, the recipient, current price, tax, quantity, delivery date and payment terms would all need approval before an order is placed.

Thank you for reviewing this practice request. We will follow up through an authorised business channel if this were a real transaction.

Yours faithfully,

Amaka Okorie
Practice Coordinator
Fresh Bites (fictional)
```

When rebuilding this text, make the sender and recipient blocks easy to scan, distinguish the subject line, keep paragraphs short, and use the paragraph tools rather than repeated spaces or tabs to position text. Keep the fictional-data notice with any copied example.
