# Practice content: handling WhatsApp orders

Use this fictional text to build a clear standard operating procedure in Google Docs. It is not a real business policy or a record of actual customer data. Add a title, owner, review date, headings, table of contents and a checklist in your finished document.

## Purpose

Help the Fresh Bites team receive, confirm, prepare and hand over a fictional order consistently. The team must confirm what the customer requested before preparing food. If a detail is missing or unclear, ask a follow-up question rather than guessing.

## Scope and roles

This practice procedure covers the counter team, kitchen team and rider role. A counter team member checks the message and records a fictional order summary. A kitchen team member confirms availability and preparation status. A rider receives only the delivery information needed for an assigned practice order. The owner resolves exceptions.

## Procedure

1. **Read the request.** Check the item, quantity, pickup or delivery choice, requested time and any dietary note. Do not copy unnecessary personal details into the practice tracker.
2. **Confirm availability.** Ask the kitchen role to confirm that the requested item can be prepared. If an item is unavailable, offer a clear alternative only after approval.
3. **Repeat the order.** Send a short summary with items, quantities, agreed collection or delivery window and the approved total. Ask the customer to confirm the summary.
4. **Record the order.** Add a fictional order ID, date, items, quantity, channel and status to the practice sheet. Do not put account passwords, full card numbers or private notes in the sheet.
5. **Prepare the order.** The kitchen role changes the status to `Preparing`, then `Ready` after a second check of the order summary.
6. **Hand over.** For pickup, confirm the order ID. For delivery, provide the rider only the information needed for that delivery and follow the owner's approved process.
7. **Close the record.** Mark the final status and note a short, factual exception if something changed. Do not keep practice data longer than the exercise requires.

## Escalation

Pause and ask the owner before promising an unavailable item, changing an agreed price, collecting extra personal information or sharing a sheet outside the practice team. If a message appears suspicious, do not click links or share verification codes. Use the official account or business channel already known to the team.
