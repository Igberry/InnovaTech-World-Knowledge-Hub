# Practice proposal content: Fresh Bites team lunch

Use this fictional brief to practise a concise two-page proposal in Google Docs. The organisation, details and quantities below are made up for training. Confirm every assumption with a real client before using a proposal template in paid work.

## Proposal request

A fictional office team in Yenagoa is planning a lunch for 24 people next month. It wants a simple meal option, a vegetarian alternative, bottled water and a clear pickup or delivery window. The requester has not provided a final date, budget or delivery address.

## Suggested proposal sections

### 1. Summary

Restate the request in one short paragraph. State that the date, budget and handover location are still to be confirmed. Do not imply that Fresh Bites has accepted the booking.

### 2. Proposed menu structure

- Main option: jollof rice with grilled chicken.
- Vegetarian option: jollof rice with beans and plantain.
- Side: a small portion of fried plantain.
- Drink: bottled water.

Treat these as illustrative menu options, not verified prices or guaranteed availability. Leave a clearly labelled placeholder for an approved quotation.

### 3. Service plan

List the details the requester must confirm: date, serving time, dietary restrictions, pickup or delivery, delivery area, contact method and the approved budget. Explain that preparation begins only after the final details and order are confirmed.

### 4. Timeline

| Step | Owner | Target date |
|---|---|---|
| Confirm date, menu and delivery choice | Requester and Fresh Bites | To confirm |
| Approve itemised quotation | Requester | To confirm |
| Confirm the final order summary | Fresh Bites | To confirm |
| Prepare and hand over | Fresh Bites | Confirmed order date |

### 5. Next step

Ask the requester to reply with the missing details. Do not include an invented phone number, email address, business address or payment instruction.
