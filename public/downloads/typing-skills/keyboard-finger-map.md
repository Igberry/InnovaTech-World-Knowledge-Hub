# Keyboard Finger Map

Use this as a printable QWERTY reference. Finger assignments are a starting guide; adapt them for your keyboard layout and comfort.

## Home row

| Left little | Left ring | Left middle | Left index | Right index | Right middle | Right ring | Right little |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | S | D | F, G | H, J | K | L | ; |

The index fingers rest on **F** and **J**, the two keys with raised markers. Thumbs rest lightly by the space bar.

| Z | X | C | V, B | N, M | , | . | / |

Use the opposite hand for Shift when comfortable. The placement of punctuation and symbols depends on the keyboard layout. Keep wrists neutral and avoid reaching painfully.
