## Passage

On Monday morning, the small team opened the shop and checked the day's notes. A clean list helps everyone see what is ready, what needs attention, and what should wait. The team worked carefully, wrote each item clearly, and asked a question when a detail was not certain. By lunchtime, the first orders were packed and the counter was tidy. At the end of the day, the manager reviewed the list and saved a fresh copy for tomorrow.
