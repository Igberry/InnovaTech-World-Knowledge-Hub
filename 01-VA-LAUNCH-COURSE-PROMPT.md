## 5. Week-by-Week Content Specification

> For each week, write the sections listed in section 2. The bullets below are the **required content**. Cover every bullet. Add examples and scripts, but do not drop or reorder items.

### Week 1: VA Foundations & Self-Assessment
**Goal:** Understand what a VA does, audit your skills, and commit to a niche.
**Objectives:** explain what VAs do and what clients pay for; list 3+ VA types; complete a skills self-audit; choose a niche; write a niche statement; set up email, folders and tracker.

**Session A (Day 1, 60 min), Welcome and What a VA Actually Does.** Program overview and weekly rhythm; types of VAs (general admin, executive, social media, e-commerce, tech/niche); **realistic income**: explain pricing models and **how to research current rates** (platform listings, job posts, peers), and give a **clearly labelled illustrative worked example** (e.g., hours x rate = monthly income) **without quoting real market figures or promises**; what clients actually pay for (time saved, reliability, communication); myth-busting (what VAs are not); learner Q&A on doubts. *Learner action:* come with questions.

**Quick Reference (Day 2), Skills Self-Audit Primer.** What "transferable skills" means; examples of local admin/customer-service experience translating into VA proof (e.g., running a shop's records becomes bookkeeping support; managing church programmes becomes calendar and event coordination); how to prepare 3 of your own transferable skills before Day 3.

**Session B (Day 3, 75 min), Self-Assessment and Choosing Your VA Niche.** Guided skills inventory exercise; generalist vs specialist VA (pros and cons table); common niches: social media, e-commerce, real estate, coaching/course VA; how to choose (interest, proof, demand, ability to explain it in one sentence); coaching as you draft your niche statement. *Learner action:* leave with a draft niche statement.

**Session C (Day 5, 60 min), Niche Clarity Workshop.** Focused review of the niche statement; troubleshooting when torn between niches (decision matrix); set up the work environment together (email, folders, tracker).

**Job-hunt focus:** No applications. Setup only: professional email, folder system, Application and Skills Tracker.
**Assignment:** Final Niche Statement (one paragraph: the type of VA work you do, for whom, and why you are credible) + completed Skills Self-Audit worksheet. Weights: niche statement 50%, audit 40%, setup done 10%.
**Downloads:** `skills-self-audit-worksheet.csv`, `va-niches-handout.md`, `niche-statement-template.md`, `application-tracker-template.csv`.
**Facilitator Notes (Prepare):** Skills Self-Audit worksheet, list of VA niches handout, Google Meet links for all 3 live sessions sent with calendar invites ahead of time.

### Week 2: Core Tools Every VA Must Master
**Goal:** Operate confidently in the tools clients use daily and set up your platform profiles.

**Session A (Day 1, 75 min), Google Workspace and Microsoft 365 Live Walkthrough.** Live shared-screen: Gmail/Outlook inbox management (labels, filters, delegation); Google Calendar scheduling and shared calendars; Drive/OneDrive folder structures and sharing permissions. *Learner action:* set up a real folder and calendar live alongside the mentor.

**Quick Reference (Day 2), Slack and Zoom Etiquette.** Slack basics (channels, threads, status, notifications, when to DM vs post); Zoom basics (mute, camera, background, link sharing); professional etiquette (response times, summarising calls in writing).

**Session B (Day 3, 75 min), Collaboration Tools + Time Tracking and Invoicing.** Live demo of Trello, Asana, Notion (picking up any client's system quickly); time tracking tools (Toggl, Clockify); working across time zones (a worked example with a US/UK client and a Nigerian schedule); build a simple **invoice template** together. *Also introduce payments:* Payoneer, Wise, local bank transfer for receiving international payments (verify current availability and fees; date-stamp). *Learner action:* build a personal task board and one sample invoice.

**Session C (Day 5, 60 to 75 min), Hands-On Tools Practice Lab.** Live 1:1 practice setting up a **mock client workspace** across Google Workspace, Slack and a task tool; troubleshooting.

**Job-hunt focus:** Set up (do **not** apply yet) an Upwork profile, a LinkedIn profile and an OnlineJobs.ph profile. Use the Week 1 niche statement as headline/summary.
**Assignment:** screenshots or links of the 3 completed profiles + one completed sample invoice + one day of tracked time.
**Downloads:** `invoice-template.csv`, `profile-setup-checklist-upwork.md`, `profile-setup-checklist-linkedin.md`, `profile-setup-checklist-onlinejobs.md`, `mock-client-workspace-brief.md`.
**Facilitator Notes (Prepare):** sample invoice template, profile setup checklist for each of the 3 platforms, screen-share-ready walkthroughs for both live sessions.

### Week 3: Specialized VA Skill Tracks
**Goal:** Offer basic social media, admin and customer-support services, and send your first 3 practice applications.

**Session A (Day 1, 75 min), Social Media and Content Support Live Demo.** Content scheduling tools (Buffer, Meta Business Suite, Later); build a Canva graphic together live; build a simple content calendar template. *Learner action:* build a 1-week sample content calendar in Canva during the session.

**Quick Reference (Day 2), Light Bookkeeping Basics.** What "light bookkeeping" means for a VA (expense tracking, simple spreadsheets, no accounting background needed); a sample expense tracker layout.

**Session B (Day 3, 75 min), Inbox/Calendar Management + Customer Service and CRM.** Advanced inbox triage and templated responses; complex calendar management live demo; basic CRM tools (HubSpot free tier, Notion CRM, spreadsheet CRM); professional customer-service tone and scripts. *Learner action:* draft 3 customer-service response templates + a sample expense tracker.

**Session C (Day 5, 60 to 75 min), Role-Play Lab, Real Client Scenarios.** Mentor plays the client: (1) a difficult email, (2) a scheduling conflict, (3) a customer complaint; direct feedback on tone and professionalism after each. Write each scenario brief for the learner to prepare (client background, what the client says, what a good reply achieves).

**Job-hunt focus:** Send your **first 3 real applications**. Practice-focused: build the habit of applying. Use Week 2 profiles and a basic proposal template. **Log every application** in the tracker. Start the **daily WhatsApp "applications sent" check-in**.
**Assignment:** Application Tracker showing 3 real applications (platform, role, date, status) + the content calendar, expense tracker and customer-service templates built live.
**Downloads:** `basic-proposal-template.md`, `content-calendar-template.csv`, `expense-tracker-template.csv`, `customer-service-templates.md`, `role-play-scenarios.md`.
**Facilitator Notes (Prepare):** basic proposal/application template, Application Tracker (Google Sheet), 3 realistic mock client scenarios.

### Week 4: Branding, Portfolio & Pricing
**Goal:** Create believable proof of skill, polish your profile, and set clear rates and packages.

**Session A (Day 1, 75 min), Building a VA Portfolio With No Experience (Live Build-Along).** Turn Week 1 to 3 practice work (content calendar, expense tracker, templates) into portfolio samples; **mock case-study format: Problem, Action, Result**; free portfolio tools (Notion, Canva, simple one-pager). *Learner action:* build the first version live.

**Quick Reference (Day 2), VA Keywords Recruiters Search For.** Common VA-specific keywords used in LinkedIn and platform searches (group by niche); how to use them naturally in headline, About and skills.

**Session B (Day 3, 75 min), LinkedIn Optimization + Setting Your Rate and Packages.** Live rewrite of headline and About section; **hourly vs project vs retainer** pricing (table of when to use each); realistic starting rates for local and international clients (**teach how to research; do not state market figures as facts**); packaging services (e.g., a "10-hour starter package"); build a **one-page rate sheet** together. *Learner action:* leave with an updated LinkedIn profile and a rate sheet.

**Session C (Day 5, 60 to 75 min), 1:1 Profile and Portfolio Review.** Screen-share review of the learner's own LinkedIn and portfolio; live headline/About rewrites; feedback on the rate sheet.

**Job-hunt focus:** Ramp up to **5 tailored applications** this week using the finalised profile, portfolio and rate sheet. Applications are now targeted at the chosen niche.
**Assignment:** finished portfolio link/document + updated LinkedIn + rate sheet + tracker showing 5 applications.
**Downloads:** `portfolio-case-study-template.md`, `linkedin-keyword-list-by-niche.md`, `rate-sheet-template.md` (blank fields plus one worked example **labelled illustrative only**; offer Naira and USD columns), `one-page-portfolio-outline.md`.
**Facilitator Notes (Prepare):** portfolio template options, VA-specific LinkedIn keyword list, sample rate sheets in Naira and USD.

### Week 5: Platforms, Proposals & Outreach Mastery
**Goal:** Know which platforms fit your niche, write proposals that get replies, and start daily applications.

**Session A (Day 1, 75 min), Where VA Jobs Actually Are (Live Platform Tour).** Marketplaces (Upwork, Fiverr); VA-specific agencies (Belay, Time Etc, Fancy Hands; **verify each still operates and accepts remote applicants from Nigeria, and flag any that do not**); OnlineJobs.ph; Facebook groups and LinkedIn job posts; matching platform to niche and rate expectations. *Learner action:* identify and bookmark the top 3 platforms for the niche.

**Quick Reference (Day 2), Cold Outreach Message Structure.** The basic structure of a cold message (who you are in one line, what you noticed about them, the specific help you offer, proof, one easy next step); length and tone rules.

**Session B (Day 3, 75 min), Writing Proposals That Get Replies + Cold Outreach.** Live rewrite of a generic proposal into a **proof-first, client-problem-led** one (show a before and after); a **repeatable proposal framework** (name it "The 5-Line Proposal": hook that mirrors the job post; proof; plan; availability/rate; call to action); live drafting of cold outreach for LinkedIn, email and Instagram DMs. *Learner action:* draft one new proposal and 3 cold outreach messages.

**Session C (Day 5, 60 to 75 min), 1:1 Proposal-Writing Session.** Live rewrite of the learner's own proposal; score it against the framework (give a 10-point scoring checklist); troubleshoot platform account issues. *Learner action:* bring a real job post.

**Job-hunt focus:** **Daily applications start now**: minimum 1 per day (5+ for the week) **plus 3 cold outreach messages**. The shift from practising to actively hunting. Consistency over perfection.
**Assignment:** tracker with at least 5 daily-logged applications and 3 outreach messages sent, with response status noted for each.
**Downloads:** `proposal-framework-5-line.md`, `proposal-scoring-checklist.md`, `cold-outreach-templates.md` (LinkedIn, email, Instagram), `platform-and-agency-list.md` (with links only to sites you verified), `whatsapp-check-in-prompts.md`.
**Facilitator Notes (Prepare):** list of VA-friendly platforms and agencies with links, cold outreach templates, WhatsApp check-in prompts.

### Week 6: Active Job Hunt Sprint
**Goal:** Perform well when a client responds, hold your rate, and avoid scams.

**Session A (Day 1, 75 min), Handling Interviews and Trial Tasks.** What VA clients typically ask; how to answer "why should I hire you" using the niche statement; **paid vs unpaid trial tasks** (a clear policy the learner can state); presenting sample work confidently; live Q&A on real leads. *Learner action:* bring any real interview requests or leads.

**Quick Reference (Day 2), Scam Client Red Flags.** Common patterns: upfront payment for "training materials", cheque/overpayment fraud, unpaid "trial" work disguised as real projects; a printable red-flag checklist; what to do if unsure.

**Session B (Day 3, 75 min), Negotiating Rate and Scope + Spotting Scam Clients.** Live scripting of responses to lowball offers; defining scope clearly before starting; vetting a client (checks to run); safe payment practices. *Learner action:* write a personal rate-negotiation script.

**Session C (Day 5, 75 to 90 min), Mock Interview and Trial-Task Walkthrough.** Full live mock interview (mentor plays the client); structured 1:1 feedback with a **scoring rubric**; walkthrough of a sample trial task under time pressure; review scored feedback immediately.

**Job-hunt focus:** Full daily application sprint continues (1+ per day). **Schedule and attend any interviews or trial tasks offered.** Highest-intensity week.
**Assignment:** tracker updated daily + short note on any interviews/trial tasks attended and how they went.
**Downloads:** `interview-question-bank.md`, `mock-interview-scoring-rubric.md`, `sample-trial-task.md`, `scam-red-flag-checklist.md`, `rate-negotiation-scripts.md`.
**Facilitator Notes (Prepare):** mock interview question bank, personal scoring rubric, sample trial task exercise, scam red-flag checklist handout. Put **model answers** in `_facilitator/virtual-assistant/`.

### Week 7: Interview Mastery, Onboarding & Graduation
**Goal:** Start a client relationship professionally, think beyond client #1, and graduate with a career package.

**Session A (Day 1, 60 min), Onboarding Your First Client the Right Way.** First-week checklist (access setup, communication expectations, first deliverable); ask clarifying questions early instead of guessing; set a weekly check-in rhythm. *Learner action:* draft a personal client-onboarding checklist.

**Quick Reference (Day 2), Client Check-In Message Examples.** Examples of proactive check-ins (start of week plan, mid-week update, end-of-week summary, "I noticed a problem" message).

**Session B (Day 3, 75 min), Communication Habits and Turning One Client Into a Sustainable Career.** Over-communicating in the first month; asking for feedback and testimonials; diversifying income across 2 to 3 clients; continued learning paths to raise rates over time; goal-setting for the next 90 days. *Learner action:* set a personal 90-day client/income goal.

**Session C (Day 5, 90 to 120 min, in person), Final Mock Interviews and Graduation.** One final mock interview if the learner has no confirmed client/interview yet; personal recap of the 7-week journey; **certificate of completion presented 1:1**; optional introductions to the mentor's network for referrals. *Learner action:* attend in person if possible; bring current application status for a final 1:1 review.

**Job-hunt focus:** Close out: confirm a first client/job, **or** leave with a fully active pipeline and a concrete next-2-weeks plan. **Success is measured by momentum.**
**Final Deliverable: the VA Career Package:** Niche Statement (Wk 1), 3 live profiles (Wk 2), Portfolio + Rate Sheet (Wk 4), full Application Tracker log (Wk 3 to 6), and either a signed client/job **or** a documented next-steps plan.
**Downloads:** `client-onboarding-checklist.md`, `client-check-in-message-examples.md`, `ninety-day-goal-template.md`, `va-career-package-checklist.md`.
**Facilitator Notes (Prepare):** certificate template (blue and white branded; **do not use gold**), a simple graduation venue (can be informal: a cafe or office), optional introduction/referral contacts, final mock-interview slot if needed.

## 7. Course-Specific Rules

- **No income promises.** Never state typical VA earnings as fact. Teach how to research rates and calculate income.
- **Verify every named platform, agency and tool** (existence, whether it accepts applicants from Nigeria, current free tiers) and date-stamp what you state. List anything you could not verify in the handoff report.
- **Scam awareness** is part of Weeks 5 to 7. Make it concrete and practical.
- **Do not publish** the Training Commitment Agreement, commitment-fee options, or any pricing.
- All sample data (clients, businesses, invoices) must be **fictional**.
- Keep the learner companion **second-person and encouraging**, and keep scripts short enough to use in real life.
